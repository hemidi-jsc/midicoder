# Telehealth Platform - Universal-Fully Brief

## 1. Product Context

### Product Name

TeleCare Connect - Virtual Healthcare Platform

### Product Type

Comprehensive Telehealth and Virtual Care Platform enabling remote patient-provider consultations, digital care delivery, and integrated healthcare workflows for modern healthcare organizations.

### Target Market

- **Primary Markets:** North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific with focus on markets with established telehealth reimbursement frameworks
- **Customer Segments:**
    - Healthcare providers (independent practices, multi-specialty groups)
    - Health systems and integrated delivery networks
    - Insurance companies and health plans
    - Employer-sponsored health benefits programs
    - Government healthcare programs (Medicaid managed care, VA networks)
- **Organization Sizes:** 10-10,000+ providers, 1,000-5,000,000+ patients
- **Use Cases:** Primary care, specialty consultations, mental health/behavioral health, chronic care management, post-acute care follow-up, urgent care, pediatrics, women's health

### Problem Statement

Telehealth organizations and healthcare providers face unique challenges in delivering safe, effective, and compliant virtual care:

1. **Virtual Care Delivery:** Providing secure, high-quality video consultations that replicate the in-person clinical experience while maintaining patient privacy and clinical safety. Video quality, audio clarity, and platform reliability directly impact diagnostic accuracy and patient satisfaction.

2. **Clinical Integration:** Connecting telehealth encounters with existing Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems to ensure complete patient records, enable clinical decision support, and avoid duplicate documentation. Fragmented workflows between telehealth and EHR increase provider burden and risk of errors.

3. **Patient Access and Digital Divide:** Ensuring all patients can access virtual care regardless of technology access, digital literacy, or disabilities. Patients may lack broadband, smartphones, or computer skills needed for virtual visits, creating inequities in care access.

4. **Regulatory Compliance:** Navigating complex healthcare regulations including HIPAA privacy and security requirements, state-specific telehealth laws, medical licensure verification, controlled substance prescribing restrictions, and reimbursement parity requirements. Non-compliance risks significant penalties and legal liability.

5. **Cross-State Care Delivery:** Managing provider licensure across multiple states, varying state telehealth regulations, insurance network authorization in different jurisdictions, and location verification for both patient and provider at time of service.

6. **Reimbursement Complexity:** Managing complex insurance billing for telehealth services including payer-specific telehealth policies, CPT code variations for virtual visits, modifier requirements (95, GT, GO, GP), place of service codes (02, 10), and varying coverage for different specialties and patient populations.

7. **Scheduling and Workflow Management:** Coordinating virtual appointments across providers with varying availability, managing time zones for cross-state care, handling technical issues that delay visits, and integrating with existing practice scheduling systems.

8. **Clinical Documentation:** Ensuring comprehensive documentation for virtual visits that supports medical necessity, coding requirements, quality measures, and continuity of care. Virtual visits present unique documentation challenges for physical examination findings.

9. **e-Prescribing and Medication Management:** Integrating electronic prescribing with telehealth encounters, managing controlled substance prescribing restrictions (Ryan Haight Act exemptions), and ensuring medication safety in virtual settings.

10. **Patient Engagement and Adherence:** Keeping patients engaged in virtual care programs, improving appointment attendance compared to in-person care, supporting medication adherence through virtual monitoring, and maintaining therapeutic relationships remotely.

### Solution Overview

TeleCare Connect provides a comprehensive, integrated telehealth platform that enables healthcare organizations to deliver secure, compliant, and effective virtual care:

- **Virtual Visit Platform:** HIPAA-compliant HD video consultations with audio-only backup, secure messaging, screen sharing, and digital exam support (patient self-exam guidance, peripheral device integration)

- **Provider Portal:** Clinical dashboard with schedule management, patient queue, virtual exam room, clinical documentation tools, order entry, care plan management, and performance analytics

- **Patient Portal:** Web and mobile applications for appointment scheduling, virtual visit access, health records viewing, secure messaging, prescription refills, bill pay, and health education

- **EHR Integration:** Bidirectional integration with major EHR systems (Epic, Cerner, athenahealth, eClinicalWorks) for patient demographics, clinical documentation, problem lists, medication lists, allergy lists, lab results, and imaging reports

- **Revenue Cycle Management:** Telehealth-specific medical coding support, insurance eligibility verification, claims generation and submission, remittance processing, denial management, and patient billing

- **Clinical Decision Support:** Evidence-based order sets, medication interaction checking, clinical guidelines, quality measure support, and risk stratification alerts

- **Care Coordination:** Referral management, care team collaboration tools, task assignment, care plan sharing, and transition of care documentation

- **Analytics and Reporting:** Utilization analytics, quality measure reporting, financial performance dashboards, patient outcomes tracking, and operational metrics

- **Mobile Applications:** Native iOS and Android apps for patients and providers enabling telehealth access from any device with offline capabilities for forms and documentation

### Business Model

- **Licensing Model:** Per-provider, per-month subscription with tiered pricing based on organization size, module selection, and visit volume
- **Implementation Services:** Professional services for configuration, EHR integration, workflow design, training, and go-live support
- **Ongoing Support:** 24/7 clinical and technical help desk, platform updates, compliance updates, and best practices consulting
- **Transaction Fees:** Optional per-visit pricing model for smaller practices or variable volume organizations
- **Enterprise Licensing:** Site-wide licensing for health systems with volume discounts and dedicated account management

- **EHR-Built Telehealth:** Epic Hyperspace Telehealth, Cerner PowerChart Telehealth, athenaTelehealth
- **Differentiators:** Deep EHR integration, comprehensive revenue cycle support, specialty-specific workflows, superior user experience, advanced analytics, mobile-first design
- **Market Position:** Comprehensive telehealth platform targeting mid-market to enterprise healthcare organizations seeking to build or enhance virtual care capabilities

### KPI Calculation Methodology

**Patient CSAT Score:** Sum of post-visit satisfaction survey ratings (1-5 scale) / Number of completed surveys × 100, targeting ≥90% rating of 4 or 5 stars.

**Connection Success Rate:** (Number of successfully connected visits / Number of scheduled visits) × 100, excluding patient-initiated cancellations.

**Claim Acceptance Rate:** (Number of claims paid on first submission / Total claims submitted) × 100, tracked by payer and service type.

**No-Show Rate:** (Number of missed appointments / Total scheduled appointments) × 100, excluding provider-cancelled appointments.

**Provider Utilization:** (Number of completed visits / Number of available appointment slots) × 100, calculated per provider and aggregated.

**Cost per Visit:** (Total platform costs including licensing, support, infrastructure / Total completed visits) for the measurement period.

## 4. Core User Journeys (15+)

### J01: New Patient Registration and First Virtual Visit

**Persona:** Adult Patient seeking primary care

**Preconditions:**

- Patient referred to telehealth service or self-enrolling
- Patient has basic technology (smartphone, tablet, or computer with camera)
- Patient has insurance information or self-pay capability

**Journey Steps:**

1. **Discovery and Enrollment**
    - Patient accesses telehealth platform via web or mobile app
    - Views services offered, providers available, and insurance accepted
    - Clicks "New Patient" or "Get Started"
    - Selects language preference

2. **Account Creation**
    - Enters email address or phone number
    - Creates secure password with password strength indicator
    - Completes multi-factor authentication setup (SMS or authenticator app)
    - Receives verification code and completes verification

3. **Patient Registration**
    - Enters personal information (name, DOB, gender, address, phone, email)
    - Uploads or enters insurance information (member ID, group number, payer)
    - System performs real-time eligibility verification
    - Enters emergency contact information
    - Uploads ID or insurance card photos (optional)

4. **Health Intake**
    - Completes medical history questionnaire
    - Enters current medications (with photo capture option)
    - Enters known allergies and reactions
    - Reports current health problems and conditions
    - Rates overall health status
    - Uploads recent lab results or medical records (optional)

5. **Consent and Agreements**
    - Reviews and signs telehealth informed consent
    - Reviews and signs HIPAA privacy notice
    - Reviews financial responsibility statement
    - Acknowledges emergency services disclaimer
    - Consents to treatment and data use

6. **Appointment Scheduling**
    - Selects reason for visit (symptom-based or condition-based)
    - Views available providers and time slots
    - Filters by specialty, language, gender preference if needed
    - Selects appointment time and date
    - Receives confirmation with visit link and instructions

7. **Pre-Visit Preparation**
    - Receives SMS and email confirmation within 5 minutes
    - Receives pre-visit reminder 24 hours before appointment
    - Completes pre-visit health questionnaire
    - Enters vital signs if available (BP, temperature, weight)
    - Reviews technology requirements and tests connection

8. **Visit Check-In**
    - Receives check-in notification 15 minutes before visit
    - Clicks link to enter virtual waiting room
    - Confirms location (state) for licensure verification
    - Reviews and updates health information if needed
    - Waits in virtual waiting room with wait time estimate

9. **Virtual Visit**
    - Provider joins video session
    - Provider confirms patient identity and location
    - Conducts video consultation with assessment
    - Provider documents encounter in EHR
    - Provider may request patient self-exam with guidance
    - Provider discusses diagnosis and treatment plan

10. **Post-Visit Actions**
    - Provider sends prescriptions to pharmacy (if applicable)
    - Provider orders labs or referrals (if applicable)
    - Provider schedules follow-up appointment
    - Visit ends and patient receives post-visit survey

11. **Post-Visit Follow-Up**
    - Patient receives visit summary within 2 hours
    - Patient can view instructions, prescriptions, lab orders
    - Patient completes satisfaction survey
    - Patient portal shows visit in health records
    - Patient can send follow-up messages to provider

**Success Criteria:**

- Patient completes registration in <10 minutes
- Eligibility verification completes in <2 minutes
- Patient receives confirmations within 5 minutes
- Visit connection success rate >98%
- Patient completes visit with all post-visit actions

**Alternative Paths:**

- Patient without insurance: Self-pay pricing displayed, payment collected upfront
- Patient with technology issues: Audio-only visit option offered
- Patient needing in-person care: Provider recommends and facilitates referral
- Urgent symptoms: Triage to urgent care or emergency services

5. **Post-Visit**

    - Follow-up appointment scheduled
    - Patient education materials provided

**Success Criteria:**

- Pre-visit data available before visit starts
- Visit focused on active management, not data collection
- Treatment adjustments made based on RPM data
- Patient understands next steps

### J05: e-Prescribing During Virtual Visit

**Persona:** Provider prescribing medication during telehealth visit

**Preconditions:**

- Active virtual visit with patient
- Provider has determined medication is appropriate
- Patient has selected preferred pharmacy

**Journey Steps:**

1. **Medication Selection**
    - Provider opens e-prescribing module from visit
    - Searches for medication by name or condition
    - Reviews patient's medication history for interactions
    - Reviews allergy list for conflicts

2. **Prescription Details**
    - Provider enters dosage, frequency, duration
    - Provider enters refills as appropriate
    - Provider adds special instructions if needed
    - System checks for drug-drug and drug-allergy interactions

3. **Controlled Substance Check**
    - If controlled substance, system checks:
        - Provider DEA registration and state authorization
        - State controlled substance prescribing rules
        - Patient controlled substance history
        - Required two-factor authentication for EPCS

4. **Pharmacy Selection**
    - Provider selects patient's preferred pharmacy
    - Or enters new pharmacy information
    - System verifies pharmacy is in network

5. **Prescription Transmission**
    - Provider reviews prescription details
    - Provider signs and transmits prescription
    - System sends to pharmacy via Surescripts or equivalent
    - Patient receives notification that prescription was sent

6. **Documentation**
    - Prescription automatically documented in encounter note
    - Added to patient's medication list
    - Included in visit summary to patient

**Success Criteria:**

- No drug interactions missed
- Controlled substance rules followed
- Prescription received by pharmacy within 5 minutes
- Patient notified promptly

**Alternative Paths:**

## 7. Domain Rules and Invariants (15+)

### INV01: Provider Must Be Licensed in Patient's State

**Rule:** A provider cannot conduct a telehealth visit with a patient unless the provider holds an active, unrestricted medical license in the state where the patient is physically located at the time of the visit.

**Enforcement:**

- Patient location verified at check-in
- Provider licenses validated before scheduling
- System blocks visit if license not valid for patient state
- Interstate Medical Licensure Compact credentials accepted

**Exception:** Federal facilities (VA, Indian Health Service) may have different rules; disaster waivers may temporarily modify requirements.

---

### INV02: Telehealth Consent Required Before First Visit

**Rule:** Patients must complete and sign a telehealth informed consent before their first telehealth visit.

**Enforcement:**

- Consent presented during registration
- Consent cannot be bypassed
- Visit blocked if consent not completed
- Consent version tracked and managed

**Exception:** Emergency situations may allow deferred consent with documentation.

---

### INV03: Insurance Verification Required Before Visit (When Applicable)

**Rule:** For patients using insurance, eligibility must be verified before the visit when possible.

**Enforcement:**

- Eligibility check at scheduling or check-in
- Visit can proceed but flagged if verification fails
- Patient notified of potential responsibility
- Denial risk assessed

**Exception:** Self-pay patients; emergency visits.

---

### INV04: Video Recording Requires Explicit Patient Consent

**Rule:** Telehealth video sessions cannot be recorded without explicit, documented patient consent.

**Enforcement:**

- Recording consent separate from treatment consent
- Both parties notified when recording active
- Recording can be stopped by either party
- Recorded sessions stored securely with access controls

**Exception:** Court-ordered recording; quality improvement with IRB approval and de-identification.

---

### INV05: Provider Cannot Be Double-Booked

**Rule:** A provider cannot have two active appointments at the same time.

**Enforcement:**

- Schedule validation before booking
- Overlapping appointments blocked
- Buffer times respected
- Emergency overrides require authorization

**Exception:** Care coordination calls; patient education sessions outside appointment time.

---

### INV06: Prescription Requires Active Encounter

**Rule:** E-prescriptions must be linked to an active telehealth encounter or established patient relationship.

**Enforcement:**

- Encounter required before prescribing
- Encounter documented before prescription transmitted
- Prescription documented in encounter note
- Established relationship documented for refill requests

**Exception:** Standing orders; medication management without live visit (per payer rules).

---

### INV07: Lab Order Requires Active Encounter or Care Management

**Rule:** Laboratory orders must be associated with an active encounter or chronic care management.

**Enforcement:**

- Encounter or CCM enrollment required
- Order linked to encounter documentation
- Medical necessity documented
- Results routed to ordering provider

**Exception:** Screening programs; public health requirements.

---

### INV08: Clinical Documentation Required for Billing

**Rule:** Encounters billed to insurance must have complete clinical documentation supporting medical necessity.

**Enforcement:**

- Documentation required before claim submission
- Medical necessity documented (chief complaint, assessment, plan)
- Documentation completeness validated
- Unsigned notes flagged

**Exception:** None for insurance billing; self-pay may have different requirements.

---

### INV09: Provider Credentials Must Be Current

**Rule:** Provider credentials (license, DEA, NPI, board certification) must be current and valid.

**Enforcement:**

- Credential expiration tracking
- Alerts 90 days before expiration
- Provider blocked from scheduling if expired
- Re-credentialing workflow

**Exception:** Grace periods per state law; administrative extensions documented.

---

### INV10: Visit Cannot Start Without Patient Check-In

**Rule:** Patients must complete check-in before a visit can begin.

**Enforcement:**

- Check-in required before provider can admit
- Location verified at check-in
- Consent verified at check-in
- Check-in time recorded

**Exception:** Provider-initiated calls; emergency situations.

---

### INV11: Controlled Substances Require Enhanced Authentication

**Rule:** Prescribing controlled substances requires two-factor authentication (EPCS requirements).

**Enforcement:**

- Two-factor authentication required for controlled substances
- Prescriber identity verified
- Patient identity verified
- Enhanced audit trail maintained

**Exception:** None; DEA requirement for all controlled substance e-prescribing.

---

### INV12: Location Verification Required for Each Visit

**Rule:** Patient physical location must be verified at the time of each telehealth visit.

**Enforcement:**

- Location confirmed at check-in
- Location documented in encounter
- State change detected and handled
- Provider notified of patient location

**Exception:** Audio-only visits may have different requirements per state.

---

### INV13: Emergency Situations Require Appropriate Triage

**Rule:** Patients with emergency symptoms must be triaged appropriately and directed to emergency services.

**Enforcement:**

- Triage questions identify emergency symptoms
- Emergency protocols activated
- Patient directed to call 911 or go to ER
- Documentation of emergency guidance

**Exception:** None; patient safety priority.

---

### INV14: Minor Patients Require Parental Consent

**Rule:** Minors require parental or guardian consent for telehealth visits.

**Enforcement:**

- Parental consent verified for minors
- Parent/guardian present for visit (with exceptions)
- Minor-specific consents (reproductive health, mental health per state law)

**Exception:** Emancipated minors; state-specific minor consent laws (reproductive health, substance use, mental health).

---

### INV15: Audit Trail Cannot Be Modified

**Rule:** Audit trail entries cannot be modified or deleted after creation.

**Enforcement:**

- Audit logs immutable
- Tamper-evident storage
- Access to audit logs controlled
- Audit log integrity verified

**Exception:** None; audit trail integrity critical for compliance.

## 11. Roles and Permissions

### Roles

#### R01: Patient

**Description:** Individual receiving telehealth services
**Capabilities:** Schedule visits, attend virtual visits, view records, message providers, manage profile

#### R02: Patient Family Member

**Description:** Authorized family member or caregiver
**Capabilities:** Limited access to patient records (based on consent), communicate on behalf of patient

#### R03: Primary Care Provider

**Description:** Physician or NP providing primary care via telehealth
**Capabilities:** Conduct visits, document, prescribe, order labs, access full patient records

#### R04: Specialist Provider

**Description:** Specialist physician or NP providing specialty care
**Capabilities:** Conduct specialty visits, document, prescribe within scope, access relevant patient records

#### R05: Behavioral Health Provider

**Description:** Therapist, counselor, or psychiatrist providing behavioral health services
**Capabilities:** Conduct therapy sessions, document, prescribe (if psychiatrist), access behavioral health records

#### R06: Nurse Practitioner

**Description:** NP providing telehealth services
**Capabilities:** Conduct visits, document, prescribe within scope, order labs

#### R07: Physician Assistant

**Description:** PA providing telehealth services
**Capabilities:** Conduct visits, document, prescribe within scope, order labs

#### R08: Registered Nurse

**Description:** RN providing nursing services or care coordination
**Capabilities:** Nursing assessments, care coordination, limited prescribing (per state)

#### R09: Medical Assistant

**Description:** MA providing administrative and clinical support
**Capabilities:** Patient check-in, vital signs, scheduling, limited documentation

#### R10: Care Coordinator

**Description:** Staff member coordinating patient care
**Capabilities:** Patient communication, scheduling, care coordination tasks

#### R11: Scheduling Coordinator

**Description:** Staff member managing appointments
**Capabilities:** Schedule management, patient communication, waitlist management

#### R12: Billing Specialist

**Description:** Staff member managing billing and claims
**Capabilities:** Claims management, eligibility verification, payment processing

#### R13: Customer Service

**Description:** Staff member providing patient support
**Capabilities:** Patient communication, technical support, account management

#### R14: Clinical Administrator

**Description:** Administrator managing clinical operations
**Capabilities:** Provider management, quality oversight, clinical reporting

#### R15: System Administrator

**Description:** Administrator managing system configuration
**Capabilities:** User management, system configuration, technical oversight

#### R16: Compliance Officer

**Description:** Officer managing compliance requirements
**Capabilities:** Compliance monitoring, audit access, policy management

#### R17: Quality Manager

**Description:** Manager overseeing quality improvement
**Capabilities:** Quality metrics, reporting, improvement initiatives
