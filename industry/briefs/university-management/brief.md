### Problem Statement

Higher education institutions operate in an increasingly complex environment with competing pressures from multiple stakeholders:

**Student Lifecycle Management Challenges:**
Universities must manage students through a multi-year journey from prospective applicant through alumni status. This includes handling tens of thousands of applications annually, coordinating enrollment across multiple terms, tracking degree progress through complex requirement structures, and maintaining lifelong engagement after graduation. Legacy systems often create silos between admission, academic, financial aid, and alumni functions, resulting in fragmented student experiences and duplicated data entry.

**Academic Program Complexity:**
Modern universities offer hundreds of degree programs with intricate requirements including majors, minors, concentrations, certificates, and joint degrees. Course prerequisites create complex dependency graphs that must be validated during registration. Degree audit systems must track progress across multiple dimensions: general education requirements, major requirements, elective requirements, residency requirements, and grade requirements. Academic calendars vary by term type (semester, quarter, trimester) with different add/drop periods, grade submission deadlines, and registration windows.

**Financial Aid Administration:**
Federal, state, institutional, and private funding sources create a complex financial aid ecosystem. Title IV compliance requires strict adherence to Department of Education regulations including satisfactory academic progress monitoring, cost of attendance calculations, and disbursement timing. Financial aid offices must coordinate with federal systems (FAFSA, NSLDS), state grant programs, scholarship databases, and institutional billing systems while ensuring accurate communication with students about aid packages.

**Faculty and Research Management:**
Research universities must support complex faculty roles that span teaching, research, and service. Faculty appointment systems track ranks (assistant, associate, full professor), appointment types (tenure-track, teaching-track, research-track), and divided appointments across departments. Research administration requires grant lifecycle management from proposal submission through closeout, including IRB/IACUC compliance for human subjects and animal research, procurement for research equipment, and reporting to funding agencies.

**Regulatory and Accreditation Compliance:**
Universities face regulatory oversight from multiple sources: Department of Education for Title IV eligibility, regional and programmatic accreditors for academic quality, state authorization agencies for interstate operations, SEVIS for international student management, and state attorney generals for consumer protection. Compliance failures can result in loss of federal funding, accreditation sanctions, or legal penalties.

### Solution Overview

EduCore University provides an integrated cloud platform that unifies all aspects of higher education operations:

**Student Information System (SIS):**
Central student record from application through alumni status. Supports multi-campus enrollment, transfer credit evaluation, academic standing calculations, and student self-service portals. Integration with LMS platforms for roster and grade synchronization.

**Admissions Management:**
End-to-end application processing including online applications, document collection, recommendation letters, application review workflows, committee management, decision processing, and enrollment management dashboards.

**Academic Management:**
Program catalog with degree requirements, course catalog with prerequisites and restrictions, class scheduling with room and instructor assignment, registration with hold management, degree audit with what-if planning, and grade management with transcript generation.

**Financial Aid and Billing:**
FAFSA integration, aid package building, scholarship management, Title IV compliance, disbursement processing, tuition billing, payment processing, and account management with payment plan options.

**Faculty and Research Administration:**
Faculty profiles with teaching load, research interests, and publication tracking. Grant management for proposal submission, award administration, budget tracking, expense reporting, and closeout. IRB/IACUC submission and review workflows.

**Registrar Services:**
Class scheduling, enrollment management, grade processing, transcript services (unofficial online, official ordered), degree certification, and reporting to federal and state agencies (IPEDS, NCES).

**Alumni and Development:**
Alumni record management, event management, giving and donation processing, campaign management, endowment tracking, and career outcome tracking for accreditation reporting.

**Analytics and Reporting:**
Standard reports for operational needs (enrollment, retention, graduation), compliance reports (IPEDS, Title IV, accreditation), custom report builder, and predictive analytics for retention intervention.

## 4. Core User Journeys

### J01: Admission Application Journey

**Journey Name:** Prospective Student Application to Enrollment

**Primary Persona:** P01 - Prospective Student

**Trigger Event:** Student decides to apply to university

**Preconditions:**

- Student meets basic eligibility (high school diploma or equivalent)
- Application period is open
- Application portal is accessible

**Postconditions:**

- Application submitted and under review
- Student receives admission decision
- If admitted, student can accept enrollment

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. **Application Initiation**
    - Student creates account in admissions portal
    - Student selects program(s) of interest
    - Student reviews application requirements and deadlines

2. **Application Completion**
    - Student enters biographical information
    - Student enters academic history (high school, colleges)
    - Student uploads transcripts
    - Student submits standardized test scores (if required)
    - Student writes personal statement/essay
    - Student requests recommendation letters
    - Student uploads additional documents (portfolio, and other examples)

3. **Application Review**
    - System validates application completeness
    - Application assigned to admissions reviewer
    - Reviewer evaluates application against criteria
    - Additional documents requested if needed
    - Committee review if required

4. **Decision Processing**
    - Admissions decision made (admit, deny, waitlist)
    - Decision letter generated
    - Student notified via email and portal

5. **Enrollment Acceptance (if admitted)**
    - Student logs into portal
    - Student views admission offer
    - Student accepts or declines enrollment
    - Student completes enrollment checklist (deposit, housing, and other examples)
    - Student assigned to orientation

**Alternative Paths:**

- Application incomplete: Student notified of missing items
- Additional review needed: Application flagged for committee
- Waitlist: Student notified and may be admitted subsequently
- Denied: Student receives denial letter with appeal option

**Success Criteria:**

- Application completed without errors
- Decision communicated within stated timeframe
- Student experience rated 4+ on satisfaction survey

**Metrics Tracked:**

- Application completion rate
- Average time to decision
- Yield rate (admitted students who enroll)
- Application satisfaction score

### J02: Course Registration Journey

**Journey Name:** Student Course Registration

**Primary Persona:** P02 - Undergraduate Student

**Trigger Event:** Registration period opens for student's class standing

**Preconditions:**

- Student is in good academic standing
- Student is in good financial standing
- Student has completed advising requirement (if applicable)
- Registration period is open
- Student has registration access

**Postconditions:**

- Student enrolled in selected courses
- Tuition charges assessed
- Student can view enrolled courses

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. **Pre-Registration Planning**
    - Student views degree audit to identify remaining requirements
    - Student reviews course catalog for options
    - Student checks prerequisites for desired courses
    - Student meets with advisor (if required)
    - Student creates preliminary schedule

2. **Registration Execution**
    - Student logs into registration system when access opens
    - Student searches for available courses
    - Student adds courses to shopping cart
    - Student reviews cart for conflicts (time, prerequisite, capacity)
    - Student submits registration
    - System processes registration and assigns seat numbers

3. **Post-Registration**
    - Student views confirmed schedule
    - System sends confirmation email
    - Tuition charges assessed to student account
    - Rosters sent to instructors

**Alternative Paths:**

- Course full: Student joins waitlist or selects alternative
- Prerequisite not met: System blocks registration, student requests override
- Time conflict: System warns student, student resolves
- Hold on account: Student must resolve hold before registration
- Maximum credits exceeded: Student requests overload permission

**Success Criteria:**

- Student successfully registers for intended courses
- Registration completed without system errors
- Student can view accurate schedule

**Metrics Tracked:**

- Registration completion rate
- Average registration time
- Course fill rates
- Waitlist conversion rate

### J03: Financial Aid Application Journey

**Journey Name:** Student Financial Aid from Application to Disbursement

**Primary Persona:** P02 - Undergraduate Student

**Trigger Event:** Financial aid year begins (typically October for US)

**Preconditions:**

- Student is enrolled or planning to enroll
- Student has valid Social Security Number (or Alien Registration Number)
- FAFSA website is accessible

**Postconditions:**

- Financial aid package created and offered to student
- Student accepts aid
- Aid disburses to student account

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. **FAFSA Submission**
    - Student completes FAFSA at fafsa.gov
    - FAFSA processed by federal system
    - Student receives Student Aid Report (SAR)
    - FAFSA data transmitted to university

2. **Aid Package Creation**
    - System receives FAFSA data
    - System calculates Expected Family Contribution (EFC)
    - Financial aid officer reviews package
    - Aid package built based on eligibility and availability
    - Package includes federal grants, loans, work-study, institutional aid

3. **Package Notification**
    - Student receives award letter
    - Student logs into portal to review package
    - Student accepts or declines each aid component
    - Student completes required documents (Master Promissory Note, Entrance Counseling)

4. **Verification (if selected)**
    - Student notified of verification selection
    - Student submits verification documents
    - Aid office reviews and makes corrections
    - Package updated if needed

5. **Disbursement**
    - Student enrolls in required minimum credits
    - System confirms enrollment status
    - Aid disburses to student account
    - Credit applied to tuition and fees
    - Refund issued if excess aid

**Alternative Paths:**

- Verification required: Additional documentation needed
- Professional judgment: Appeal for特殊 circumstances
- Satisfactory Academic Progress issue: Aid suspended
- Loan limit exceeded: Adjusted package

**Success Criteria:**

- Aid package accurate on first pass
- Disbursement occurs before billing due date
- Student understands aid package

**Metrics Tracked:**

- Aid accuracy rate
- Processing time
- Verification completion rate
- Student understanding score

### J04: Academic Advising Journey

**Journey Name:** Student-Academic Advisor Meeting

**Primary Persona:** P02 - Student, P05 - Academic Advisor

**Trigger Event:** Scheduled advising appointment or student-initiated meeting

**Preconditions:**

- Student has assigned advisor
- Appointment scheduled or walk-in available
- Both parties have access to student records

**Postconditions:**

- Student has clear plan for next term
- Any holds resolved
- Registration authorized if applicable

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. **Pre-Meeting Preparation**
    - Student reviews degree audit before meeting
    - Student identifies questions or concerns
    - Advisor reviews student academic record
    - Advisor notes any alerts (low GPA, missing requirements)

2. **Advising Meeting**
    - Advisor reviews current academic standing
    - Advisor and student review degree progress
    - Student discusses goals and interests
    - Advisor recommends courses for next term
    - Advisor identifies potential issues (prerequisites, sequences)
    - Advisor resolves any holds or barriers
    - Advisor documents advising notes

3. **Post-Meeting Actions**
    - Student authorized for registration if applicable
    - Advisor follows up on referrals
    - Student registers for recommended courses

**Alternative Paths:**

- Student at-risk: Referral to support services
- Graduation planning: Degree audit verification
- Major change: New requirements review
- Transfer student: Credit evaluation

**Success Criteria:**

- Student leaves with clear action plan
- Student registers for appropriate courses
- Student satisfaction with advising experience

**Metrics Tracked:**

- Advising appointment completion rate
- Student satisfaction score
- Advisor caseload balance
- Referral completion rate

### J05: Grade Submission Journey

**Journey Name:** Faculty Grade Entry and Processing

**Primary Persona:** P04 - Faculty Member

**Trigger Event:** End of term grade submission period opens

**Preconditions:**

- Faculty member has teaching assignment
- Course has enrolled students
- Grade period is open

**Postconditions:**

- Grades entered and validated
- Grades posted to student records
- Transcripts updated
- Financial aid SAP calculated

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. **Grade Entry**
    - Faculty logs into faculty portal
    - Faculty views assigned courses
    - Faculty accesses roster for each course
    - Faculty enters final grades for each student
    - Faculty submits grades

2. **Grade Validation**
    - System validates grade codes
    - System checks for incomplete students (requires contract)
    - System confirms submission

3. **Grade Processing**
    - Grades transmitted to SIS
    - GPA recalculated for affected students
    - Academic standing recalculated
    - SAP calculated for financial aid
    - Transcripts updated
    - Students notified grades available

**Alternative Paths:**

- Grade change request: Student/faculty submits change form
- Incomplete grade: Contract required, subsequently conversion
- Grade appeal: Student initiates appeal process
- Audit required: Registrar reviews grade distribution

**Success Criteria:**

## 7. Domain Rules and Invariants

### Data Integrity Rules

### INV01: Student ID Uniqueness

- **Name:** Unique Student Identifier
- **Description:** Each student must have a unique identifier across the institution
- **Formal Statement:** ∀s1, s2 ∈ Students: s1.id = s2.id → s1 = s2
- **Enforcement Point:** Compile-time (database unique constraint), Runtime (application validation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code DUPLICATE_STUDENT_ID, message "Student ID must be unique"
- **Test Cases:**
    - Attempt to create student with existing ID → Rejected
    - Create student with new ID → Accepted

### INV02: Enrollment Existence

- **Description:** A student must exist before enrollment can be created
- **Formal Statement:** ∀e ∈ Enrollments: ∃s ∈ Students: s.id = e.student_id
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (foreign key constraint)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code INVALID_STUDENT, message "Student does not exist"
- **Test Cases:**
    - Create enrollment for non-existent student → Rejected
    - Create enrollment for valid student → Accepted

### INV03: Grade Validity

- **Description:** Grades must be valid grade codes for the course type
- **Formal Statement:** ∀g ∈ Grades: g.code ∈ ValidGradeCodes(g.course_type)
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (validation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code INVALID_GRADE, message "Invalid grade code for course type"
- **Test Cases:**
    - Enter invalid grade code → Rejected
    - Enter valid grade code → Accepted

### Business Logic Rules

### INV04: Registration Standing

- **Name:** Good Standing Required for Registration
- **Description:** Students must be in good academic and financial standing to register
- **Formal Statement:** ∀r ∈ Registrations: r.student.academic_standing = GOOD ∧ r.student.financial_standing = GOOD
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (pre-registration check)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code NOT_IN_STANDING, message "Student not in good standing"
- **Test Cases:**
    - Student with academic hold attempts registration → Rejected
    - Student with financial hold attempts registration → Rejected
    - Student in good standing registers → Accepted

### INV05: Prerequisite Satisfaction

- **Name:** Prerequisites Must Be Met
- **Description:** Students must complete prerequisites before enrolling in course
- **Formal Statement:** ∀e ∈ Enrollments: ∀p ∈ Prerequisites(e.course): ∃c ∈ CompletedCourses(e.student): c.satisfies(p)
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (registration validation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code PREREQUISITE_NOT_MET, message "Prerequisite not satisfied"
- **Test Cases:**
    - Student without prerequisite registers → Rejected
    - Student with prerequisite registers → Accepted
    - Student with prerequisite in progress registers → Conditional acceptance

### INV06: Credit Hour Limits

- **Name:** Credit Hour Limits Enforced
- **Description:** Students cannot exceed maximum credit hours per term without approval
- **Formal Statement:** ∀e ∈ Enrollments: Σe.course.credits ≤ MaxCredits(e.student.standing) ∨ e.student.has_overload_approval
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (registration validation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code CREDIT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED, message "Maximum credit hours exceeded"
- **Test Cases:**
    - Student exceeds credits without approval → Rejected
    - Student within limits registers → Accepted
    - Student with overload approval exceeds limits → Accepted

### State Transition Invariants

### INV07: Application State Transitions

- **Name:** Application State Machine
- **Description:** Applications must follow valid state transitions
- **Formal Statement:** ∀a ∈ Applications: a.state ∈ ValidTransitions(a.previous_state)
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (state machine validation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION, message "Invalid application state change"
- **Valid Transitions:** Draft → Submitted → Under Review → Decision → Accepted/Rejected/Waitlisted
- **Test Cases:**
    - Draft application submitted → Accepted
    - Submitted application decision made → Accepted
    - Decision application modified → Rejected (unless appeal)

### INV08: Enrollment State Transitions

- **Name:** Enrollment State Machine
- **Description:** Enrollments must follow valid state transitions
- **Formal Statement:** ∀e ∈ Enrollments: e.state ∈ ValidTransitions(e.previous_state)
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (state machine validation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code INVALID_ENROLLMENT_STATE, message "Invalid enrollment state change"
- **Valid Transitions:** Registered → Attending → Completed/Dropped/Withdrawn
- **Test Cases:**
    - New enrollment registered → Accepted
    - Registered student drops → Accepted
    - Completed enrollment dropped → Rejected

### INV09: Grade Period Integrity

- **Name:** Grade Period Closure
- **Description:** Grades cannot be modified after grade period closes
- **Formal Statement:** ∀g ∈ Grades: g.period.closed → g.immutable = true
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (period status check)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code GRADE_PERIOD_CLOSED, message "Grade period closed for modifications"
- **Test Cases:**
    - Grade change during open period → Accepted
    - Grade change after period close → Rejected
    - Grade change with Dean's override after close → Accepted

### Temporal Invariants

### INV10: Registration Period Validity

- **Name:** Registration Within Period
- **Description:** Registrations can only occur during open registration periods
- **Formal Statement:** ∀r ∈ Registrations: RegistrationPeriod.current.status = OPEN
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (period status check)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code REGISTRATION_CLOSED, message "Registration period not open"
- **Test Cases:**
    - Registration during open period → Accepted
    - Registration before period opens → Rejected
    - Registration after period closes → Rejected

### INV11: Term Chronology

- **Name:** Term Sequence Integrity
- **Description:** Terms must follow chronological order
- **Formal Statement:** ∀t1, t2 ∈ Terms: t2.starts_after(t1) → t2.end_date > t1.end_date
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (calendar validation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code INVALID_TERM_SEQUENCE, message "Terms must be chronological"
- **Test Cases:**
    - Term added after previous term → Accepted
    - Term overlaps incorrectly → Rejected

### Threshold Invariants

### INV12: Minimum Credit Hours for Full-Time

- **Name:** Full-Time Status Threshold
- **Description:** Students must meet minimum credits for full-time status
- **Formal Statement:** ∀s ∈ Students: s.credits_enrolled ≥ MinFullTimeCredits → s.status = FULL_TIME
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (enrollment calculation)
- **Violation Handling:** Warning, status adjustment
- **Test Cases:**
    - Student enrolls in 12+ credits → Full-time status
    - Student drops below 12 credits → Part-time status

### INV13: Financial Aid SAP

- **Name:** Satisfactory Academic Progress
- **Description:** Students must maintain SAP for financial aid eligibility
- **Formal Statement:** ∀s ∈ Students: s.gpa ≥ MinGPA ∧ s.completion_rate ≥ MinRate → s.aid_eligible = true
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (SAP calculation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code SAP_NOT_MET, message "SAP requirements not met"
- **Test Cases:**
    - Student meets SAP → Aid eligible
    - Student fails SAP → Aid ineligible

### Relationship Invariants

### INV14: Faculty-Course Assignment

- **Name:** Faculty Course Assignment
- **Description:** Each course section must have at least one instructor
- **Formal Statement:** ∀c ∈ CourseSections: ∃f ∈ Faculty: f.assignments.contains(c)
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (assignment validation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code NO_INSTRUCTOR, message "Section must have instructor"
- **Test Cases:**
    - Section created without instructor → Rejected
    - Section created with instructor → Accepted

### INV15: Degree-Program Relationship

- **Name:** Degree-Program Consistency
- **Description:** Degrees must be offered by valid programs
- **Formal Statement:** ∀d ∈ Degrees: ∃p ∈ Programs: p.offers(d)
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (program validation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code INVALID_DEGREE_PROGRAM, message "Degree not offered by program"
- **Test Cases:**
    - Student applies for degree in enrolled program → Accepted
    - Student applies for degree not in program → Rejected
