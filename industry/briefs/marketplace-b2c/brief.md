# Marketplace B2C Platform - Universal-Fully Brief

## 1. Product Context

### Product Name

MarketPlace Pro - Multi-Vendor B2C Marketplace Platform

### Product Type

B2C Multi-Vendor Marketplace Platform with Two-Sided Network Economics

### Target Market

- **Primary Markets:** North America (US, Canada), Western Europe (UK, Germany, France), Southeast Asia (Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam)
- **Customer Segments:** Small to medium vendors ($50K-$10M annual revenue) and buyers seeking diverse product selection
- **Product Categories:** Consumer Electronics, Fashion & Accessories, Home & Garden, Sports & Outdoors, Beauty & Health, Books & Media

### Problem Statement

Building and operating a successful multi-vendor marketplace presents unique challenges:

1. **Two-Sided Network Effects:** Marketplaces must simultaneously attract and retain both buyers and sellers. The "chicken and egg" problem creates significant go-to-market complexity where insufficient seller inventory drives away buyers, and low buyer traffic discourages sellers.

2. **Trust and Safety Management:** Marketplaces face heightened liability and reputational risk from fraudulent sellers, counterfeit products, and payment disputes. Building trust requires sophisticated verification, monitoring, and dispute resolution systems.

3. **Complex Financial Settlements:** Managing escrow payments, commission structures, split payments to multiple vendors, chargeback allocation, and tax compliance across jurisdictions creates significant operational complexity.

4. **Vendor Onboarding and Enablement:** Sellers require intuitive tools for product listing, inventory management, order fulfillment, and performance analytics. Poor seller experience leads to churn and reduced inventory quality.

5. **Quality and Consistency:** Maintaining consistent buyer experience across diverse seller offerings requires strict quality controls, standardized listing formats, and enforcement mechanisms.

6. **Scalability Challenges:** Marketplace growth introduces exponential complexity in matching, search relevance, fraud detection, and customer support at scale.

### Solution Overview

MarketPlace Pro is a comprehensive multi-vendor marketplace platform that provides:

- **Dual-Facing Platform:** Optimized experiences for both buyers (discovery, shopping) and sellers (onboarding, operations, analytics)
- **Trust & Safety Engine:** Automated fraud detection, seller verification workflows, review moderation, and dispute resolution
- **Flexible Payment Infrastructure:** Escrow management, split payments, configurable commission models, and automated settlements
- **Seller Enablement Suite:** Self-service onboarding, product management, inventory sync, order fulfillment tools, and performance dashboards
- **Intelligent Matching:** ML-powered search and recommendations balancing relevance, seller performance, and marketplace goals
- **Commission and Payout Engine:** Configurable fee structures, automated settlement scheduling, and financial reporting
- **Multi-Currency and Multi-Language:** Global marketplace support with localized experiences

### Business Model

- **Revenue Model:**
    - **Transaction Commission:** 5-15% per transaction (category-dependent)
    - **Seller Subscription:** $29-$299/month for premium seller features
    - **Promoted Listings:** Pay-per-click and sponsored product placements
    - **Payment Processing:** 0.3-0.5% surcharge on processed transactions
    - **Value-Added Services:** Fulfillment by Marketplace, insurance, financing

- **Pricing Tiers for Sellers:**
    - **Starter:** Free listing, 10% commission, basic analytics
    - **Professional:** $29/month, 8% commission, enhanced analytics, priority support
    - **Enterprise:** $299/month, 6% commission, API access, dedicated account manager

- **Buyer Monetization:**
    - Premium membership: $9.99/month (free shipping, early access)
    - Marketplace credit top-up programs

### Go-to-Market Strategy

- **Launch Phase (Months 1-6):**
    - Seed 100 sellers in 3 focus categories (Electronics, Fashion, Home)
    - Recruit 1,000 founding buyers with exclusive early-bird offers
    - Target 10,000 transactions in first 6 months
- **Growth Phase (Months 7-18):**
    - Expand to 1,000 sellers and 50,000 active buyers
    - Launch in 3 additional countries
    - Introduce promoted listings and premium memberships

- **Scale Phase (Months 19-36):**
    - 10,000+ sellers, 500,000+ active buyers
    - Cross-border marketplace expansion
    - Marketplace-as-a-Service offering for vertical-specific deployments

## 4. Core User Journeys

### J01: Seller Onboarding and Verification

**Primary Persona:** P01 (Alex Rivera - Independent Seller)

**Trigger Event:** Seller decides to join marketplace

**Preconditions:**

- Seller has business identity documents
- Seller has bank account for payouts
- Seller has products to sell

**Postconditions:**

- Seller account is verified and active
- Seller has completed tax information
- Seller can create listings and receive orders

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. Seller visits marketplace seller registration page
2. Seller creates account with email and password
3. Seller selects seller type (Individual or Business)
4. Seller provides business information:
    - Business name and legal structure
    - Tax ID / EIN
    - Business address and contact information
5. Seller uploads verification documents:
    - Government ID (Individual) or Business License (Business)
    - Proof of address (utility bill, bank statement)
6. Seller sets up payout information:
    - Bank account details
    - Payout frequency preference
7. Seller completes tax interview (W-9 or W-8 for US)
8. System performs background and identity verification (automated + manual review)
9. Seller receives verification decision (instant or within 48 hours)
10. Upon approval, seller completes onboarding tutorial
11. Seller creates first product listing
12. Seller account is fully active

**Alternative Paths:**

- **Fast-track verification:** High-trust sellers (existing business relationships) skip manual review
- **Enterprise onboarding:** Dedicated account manager handles enterprise seller onboarding
- **Rejected onboarding:** Seller receives rejection reason and appeal process

**Success Criteria:**

- Average onboarding time < 24 hours for standard sellers
- Verification accuracy > 99% (false positive rate < 1%)
- Onboarding completion rate > 70%

**Metrics Tracked:**

- Onboarding funnel conversion rate
- Time to first listing
- Verification approval rate
- Onboarding drop-off points

### J02: Product Listing Creation

**Primary Persona:** P01 (Alex Rivera - Independent Seller)

**Trigger Event:** Seller wants to add new product to marketplace

**Preconditions:**

- Seller account is active and verified
- Seller has product information and images ready

**Postconditions:**

- Product listing is live and searchable
- Product appears in search results
- Product can be added to buyer cart

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. Seller navigates to Inventory > Add Product
2. Seller chooses listing method:
    - Create new listing
    - Match to existing catalog item
    - Bulk upload via CSV
3. For new listing, seller enters product information:
    - Product title and description
    - Category and subcategory selection
    - Brand and manufacturer
    - SKU and barcode (if applicable)
4. Seller sets pricing:
    - Base price
    - Sale price (optional, with dates)
    - Quantity discounts (optional)
5. Seller manages inventory:
    - Initial quantity
    - Inventory tracking method (marketplace or external)
    - Backorder settings
6. Seller uploads product media:
    - Primary and gallery images (minimum 1, recommended 5)
    - Product video (optional)
    - Image alt text for accessibility
7. Seller configures shipping:
    - Shipping template selection or custom rates
    - Processing time
    - Shipping restrictions
8. Seller adds product attributes:
    - Size, color, material variants (if applicable)
    - Condition (new, used, refurbished)
    - Other relevant attributes
9. Seller previews listing for accuracy
10. Seller submits for review (if required for category)
11. Listing goes live upon approval (or immediately if no review required)

**Alternative Paths:**

- **Catalog match:** Seller finds existing catalog item and lists at their price
- **Bulk upload:** Seller uploads CSV with multiple products
- **API listing:** Enterprise seller creates listings via API integration
- **Draft mode:** Seller saves draft to complete later

**Success Criteria:**

- Listing creation time < 10 minutes for single product
- Listing approval time < 24 hours for review-required categories
- Listing quality score > 80% (completeness)

**Metrics Tracked:**

- Time to create first listing
- Listing completeness rate
- Catalog match rate
- Bulk upload usage

### J06: Buyer Returns and Refunds

**Primary Persona:** P03 (Maria Santos - Buyer)

**Trigger Event:** Buyer wants to return a purchased item

**Preconditions:**

- Order was delivered
- Return is within return window
- Item meets return eligibility criteria

**Postconditions:**

- Return is authorized
- Refund is processed
- Inventory is updated
- Return reason is recorded

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. Buyer navigates to Orders in account
2. Buyer selects order containing item to return
3. Buyer clicks "Return Item" button
4. Buyer selects item(s) and quantity to return
5. Buyer selects return reason from dropdown:
    - Not as described
    - Damaged in shipping
    - Changed mind
    - Wrong item received
    - Other
6. System checks return eligibility:
    - Within return window
    - Item is returnable per seller policy
    - No prior return abuse
7. Buyer uploads photos if required (for damage claims)
8. Buyer submits return request
9. System notifies seller of return request
10. Seller reviews and responds within 48 hours:
    - Approves return
    - Denies return (with reason)
    - Offers partial refund
    - Offers replacement
11. If approved, system generates return shipping label:
    - Prepaid (seller provides) or buyer pays
12. Buyer ships item back
13. System tracks return shipment
14. Upon return receipt, seller inspects item
15. Seller confirms receipt in system
16. System processes refund:
    - To original payment method
    - Or marketplace credit
17. Buyer receives refund confirmation

**Alternative Paths:**

- **Instant approval:** Automatic for low-value, high-trust returns
- **Keep and refund:** Seller allows buyer to keep item and issues refund
- **Exchange:** Buyer requests replacement instead of refund
- **Dispute:** Buyer escalates to marketplace if seller unresponsive

**Success Criteria:**

- Return request response time < 48 hours
- Refund processing time < 5 business days
- Return completion time < 14 days

**Metrics Tracked:**

- Return rate by seller and category
- Return reason distribution
- Average time to refund
- Return authorization rate

## 7. Domain Rules and Invariants

### INV01: Order Value Integrity

- **Name:** Order Total Must Equal Sum of Line Items Plus Fees
- **Description:** The total amount of an order must always equal the sum of line item prices plus taxes, shipping, and discounts
- **Formal Statement:** `order.total = sum(order.line_items.price * quantity) + order.tax + order.shipping - order.discounts`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (before order confirmation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `ORDER_TOTAL_MISMATCH`; reject order
- **Test Cases:** Mismatched total, price change after cart add, discount application

### INV02: Inventory Cannot Be Negative

- **Name:** Available Inventory Cannot Go Negative Without Backorder Enablement
- **Description:** The available inventory for any product cannot be less than zero unless backorders are explicitly enabled
- **Formal Statement:** `∀p ∈ Products, (p.backorder_enabled OR p.available_quantity >= 0)`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (before order confirmation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `INSUFFICIENT_INVENTORY`; show out-of-stock message
- **Test Cases:** Order exceeding stock, concurrent orders, inventory adjustment

### INV03: Payment Before Fulfillment

- **Name:** Payment Must Be Secured Before Order Fulfillment
- **Description:** An order cannot be marked as fulfilled unless payment has been captured or authorized
- **Formal Statement:** `∀o ∈ Orders, o.fulfilled → (o.payment_status = captured OR o.payment_status = authorized)`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on fulfillment attempt)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `PAYMENT_NOT_SECURED`; block fulfillment
- **Test Cases:** Unpaid order fulfillment, payment failure, partial payment

### INV04: Refund Cannot Exceed Paid Amount

- **Name:** Total Refunds Cannot Exceed Original Order Total
- **Description:** The sum of all refunds for an order cannot exceed the original paid amount
- **Formal Statement:** `∀o ∈ Orders, sum(o.refunds.amount) <= o.total_paid`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (before refund creation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `OVER_REFUND`; reject refund
- **Test Cases:** Multiple partial refunds, refund after partial refund

### INV05: Seller Cannot Access Other Seller Data

- **Name:** Seller Data Isolation Must Be Enforced
- **Description:** A seller can only access their own data, not another seller's data
- **Formal Statement:** `∀s1, s2 ∈ Sellers, s1.id != s2.id → ∀d ∈ s2.data, access(s1, d) = denied`
- **Enforcement Point:** Compile-time + Runtime (all data access)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `UNAUTHORIZED_ACCESS`; log security incident
- **Test Cases:** Cross-seller order access, listing access, analytics access

### INV06: Commission Cannot Be Negative

- **Name:** Seller Commission Rate Cannot Result in Negative Marketplace Revenue
- **Description:** Commission calculations must result in non-negative marketplace revenue
- **Formal Statement:** `∀o ∈ Orders, o.marketplace_commission >= 0`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on commission calculation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `INVALID_COMMISSION`; cap at zero
- **Test Cases:** Negative commission rate, fee discount exceeding commission

### INV07: Payout Cannot Exceed Available Balance

- **Name:** Seller Payout Amount Cannot Exceed Available Funds
- **Description:** A payout amount cannot exceed the seller's available balance after holds
- **Formal Statement:** `∀p ∈ Payouts, p.amount <= seller.available_balance`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (before payout initiation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS`; reject payout
- **Test Cases:** Payout exceeding balance, pending order holds, chargeback holds

### INV08: Listing Cannot Be Live Without Required Fields

- **Name:** Product Listings Must Have Required Fields to Go Live
- **Description:** A product listing cannot be published without all required fields populated
- **Formal Statement:** `∀l ∈ Listings, l.status = live → ∀f ∈ required_fields, l.f != null`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (before listing publish)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `MISSING_REQUIRED_FIELD`; show which fields are missing
- **Test Cases:** Missing title, missing price, missing category

### INV09: Review Can Only Be Left by Verified Buyer

- **Name:** Product Reviews Must Be from Verified Purchases
- **Description:** Only users who purchased a product can leave reviews for it
- **Formal Statement:** `∀r ∈ Reviews, ∃o ∈ Orders WHERE o.buyer = r.user AND o.product = r.product`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on review submission)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `NOT_VERIFIED_BUYER`; reject review
- **Test Cases:** Non-buyer review, review for unowned product

### INV10: Dispute Resolution Must Be Final

- **Name:** Resolved Disputes Cannot Be Reopened Without New Evidence
- **Description:** Once a dispute is marked as resolved, it cannot be modified without new evidence
- **Formal Statement:** `∀d ∈ Disputes, d.status = resolved → immutable(d.decision)`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on dispute modification)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `DISPUTE_CLOSED`; require new case for escalation
- **Test Cases:** Resolution modification, reopens without new evidence

### INV11: Escrow Holds Must Be Released or Charged

- **Name:** Escrowed Funds Must Eventually Be Released or Charged
- **Description:** Funds held in escrow must be either released to seller or charged for disputes/chargebacks
- **Formal Statement:** `∀e ∈ Escrow, e.status = held → (e.released OR e.charged) within retention_period`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (periodic processing)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `ESCROW_TIMEOUT`; automatic release or escalation
- **Test Cases:** Timeout release, dispute chargeback, manual override

### INV12: Seller Cannot Modify Order After Buyer Payment

- **Name:** Order Modifications After Payment Require Buyer Consent
- **Description:** Sellers cannot modify order details (price, items) after payment without buyer consent
- **Formal Statement:** `∀o ∈ Orders, o.payment_status = paid → modifications(o) require buyer_approval`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on order modification)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `UNAUTHORIZED_MODIFICATION`; require buyer approval workflow
- **Test Cases:** Price change after payment, item substitution

## 11. Security and Access Control

### Authentication Strategy

- **Primary Mechanism:** JWT with RS256 signature
- **Token Lifetimes:** Access 15 minutes, Refresh 7 days
- **Session Management:** Redis-backed sessions with configurable timeout
- **Password Policy:** Minimum 12 chars, complexity requirements, HaveIBeenPwned check
- **2FA Support:** TOTP for sellers and admins

### Authorization Model

**Type:** RBAC with data ownership enforcement

**Roles:**

1. Platform Admin
2. Trust & Safety Admin
3. Support Agent
4. Enterprise Seller
5. Individual Seller
6. Buyer
7. Guest

### Permission Definitions

### Session Management

- **Timeout:** 30 minutes idle for buyers, 60 minutes for sellers
- **Max Sessions:** 5 per user

- **Key Management:** AWS KMS
- **PII Encryption:** Separate encryption for PII fields
