# Food Delivery Platform - Universal-Fully Brief

## 1. Product Context

### Product Name

FoodSwift Pro - Multi-Channel Food Delivery Platform

### Product Type

B2C2C Two-Sided Marketplace for Restaurant Food Delivery and Takeout

### Target Market

- **Primary Markets:** Major metropolitan areas in US, Europe, Asia-Pacific
- **Customer Segments:** Restaurants (fast casual, fine dining, QSR), consumers (home delivery, pickup), delivery drivers (gig workers)
- **Geographic Focus:** Urban and suburban areas with high restaurant density
- **Restaurant Types:** Fast casual, fine dining, quick service restaurants (QSR), cloud kitchens, specialty food vendors

### Problem Statement

Food delivery ecosystem faces unique multi-sided marketplace challenges:

1. **Real-Time Logistics Complexity:** Matching orders with drivers in real-time while optimizing routes for multiple stops, managing peak demand periods (lunch: 11:30-1:30, dinner: 5:30-8:30), and ensuring food quality during transit. Delivery times exceeding 45 minutes significantly increase cancellation rates.

2. **Restaurant Integration:** Restaurants need seamless integration with existing POS systems, kitchen display systems (KDS), and order management. Manual order entry creates errors, delays, and operational friction. 60% of restaurants still receive orders via paper or tablets.

3. **Driver Operations:** Recruiting, retaining, and optimizing gig driver workforce. Driver availability fluctuates with demand, weather, and competition. Poor driver experience leads to high turnover (average 40% monthly turnover).

4. **Quality Control:** Ensuring food arrives hot, intact, and as ordered. Temperature control, spill prevention, and accurate order assembly are critical. Food quality issues account for 25% of customer complaints.

5. **Multi-Sided Pricing:** Balancing commission rates for restaurants (15-30%), delivery fees for customers, driver pay, and platform margin. Price sensitivity on all sides requires dynamic pricing strategies.

6. **Customer Experience:** Providing real-time order tracking, accurate ETAs, easy reordering, and seamless issue resolution. Poor UX leads to low repeat rates (average 35% return rate).

7. **Regulatory Compliance:** Navigating food safety regulations, labor classifications for drivers, insurance requirements, and data privacy across jurisdictions.

8. **Demand Volatility:** Handling 10x demand during peak hours vs. off-peak, seasonal variations, and unpredictable events (bad weather = 200%+ demand).

### Solution Overview

FoodSwift Pro provides a comprehensive food delivery platform:

- **Customer App:** Restaurant discovery, menu browsing, ordering, payment, real-time tracking, reviews
- **Restaurant Portal:** Menu management, order reception, KDS integration, analytics, promotions
- **Driver App:** Order acceptance, navigation, earnings tracking, route optimization, availability management
- **Operations Console:** Real-time dispatch, support tools, quality monitoring, driver management
- **Intelligent Dispatch:** AI-powered order-to-driver matching considering proximity, capacity, ratings, and predicted acceptance
- **Real-Time Tracking:** GPS tracking with live map, driver location, ETA updates
- **Multi-Channel Support:** Web, iOS, Android, phone ordering, API integrations

### Business Model

- **Restaurant Commission:** 15-30% per order (tiered by restaurant size and volume)
- **Delivery Fees:** $2.99-$5.99 per delivery (distance-based, dynamic during peaks)
- **Service Fees:** 10-15% of order subtotal for platform use
- **Tip Processing:** 3% on tips (optional, varies by jurisdiction)
- **Subscription:** Customer membership program ($9.99/month for reduced fees)
- **Advertising:** Featured restaurant listings, promoted search results ($500-$5,000/month)
- **Enterprise:** B2B catering and corporate account programs

### Go-to-Market Strategy

- **Launch Phase (Months 1-6):** Single city launch, 100 restaurants, 200 drivers, 5,000 customers
- **Growth Phase (Months 7-18):** 10 cities, 1,000 restaurants, 5,000 drivers, 100,000 customers
- **Scale Phase (Months 19-36):** 50 cities, 5,000 restaurants, 25,000 drivers, 1M+ customers

## 4. Core User Journeys

### J01: Customer Order Placement and Tracking

**Primary Persona:** P01 (Jennifer Chen - Customer)

**Trigger Event:** Customer wants to order food for delivery

**Preconditions:**

- Customer has app installed or web access
- Customer has payment method saved
- Customer has delivery address set

**Postconditions:**

- Order is placed and confirmed
- Restaurant receives order
- Driver is assigned
- Food is delivered
- Customer rates experience

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. **App Launch:**
    - Customer opens app
    - Location detected or entered
    - Delivery address confirmed

2. **Restaurant Discovery:**
    - Browse featured restaurants
    - Search by cuisine, restaurant name
    - Filter by dietary preferences, price, rating
    - View restaurant details (menu, photos, ratings)

3. **Menu Browsing:**
    - Browse restaurant menu categories
    - View item details (description, price, calories)
    - Select item and customize (add-ons, substitutions)
    - Add to cart

4. **Cart Review:**
    - Review selected items
    - Adjust quantities
    - Apply promo codes
    - View fees and total

5. **Checkout:**
    - Confirm delivery address
    - Select delivery time (ASAP or scheduled)
    - Add special instructions
    - Select payment method
    - Add tip
    - Place order

6. **Order Confirmation:**
    - Order confirmation screen
    - Order number and estimated time
    - Push notification sent

7. **Order Tracking:**
    - Real-time status updates
    - Restaurant preparing
    - Driver assigned
    - Driver picked up order
    - Driver en route
    - Arrived at location

8. **Delivery Completion:**
    - Driver marks delivered
    - Customer receives notification
    - Optional contactless delivery

9. **Post-Delivery:**
    - Customer rates order (food quality, driver, speed)
    - Optional review and photos
    - Reorder prompt shown

**Alternative Paths:**

- **Pickup Order:** Skip driver assignment, pickup notification
- **Scheduled Delivery:** Order placed in advance for later delivery
- **Group Order:** Multiple customers contribute to single order
- **Phone Order:** Support agent places order on behalf of customer

**Success Criteria:**

- Order placement time < 3 minutes
- Order confirmation within 30 seconds
- Real-time tracking accuracy > 95%
- Customer rating ≥ 4.0

**Metrics Tracked:**

- Funnel conversion by step
- Average order placement time
- Cart abandonment rate
- Promo code usage rate

**Failure Scenarios and Handling:**

| Failure Scenario       | Expected Handling                              |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Payment fails          | Clear error, retry option, alternative payment |
| Restaurant unavailable | Show message, suggest alternatives             |
| Driver not found       | Extend search area, notify customer of delay   |
| Address not found      | Manual address entry, location picker          |

### J10: Platform Dispatch Optimization

**Primary Persona:** P05 (James Wilson - Operations Manager)

- Order is confirmed and being prepared
- System has available drivers
- Dispatch algorithm is running

**Postconditions:**

- Driver is assigned to order
- Driver accepts order
- Order moves to delivery phase

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. **Order Ready:**
    - Restaurant marks order ready
    - System triggers dispatch

2. **Driver Pool:**
    - System identifies available drivers
    - Considers proximity to restaurant
    - Considers driver capacity
    - Considers driver rating and performance

3. **Matching:**
    - Algorithm scores each driver
    - Factors: distance, acceptance rate, ETA, load
    - Top driver selected

4. **Offer:**
    - Order offer sent to driver
    - Driver has limited time to accept
    - Pay estimate shown

5. **Acceptance:**
    - Driver accepts order
    - System confirms assignment
    - Customer notified

6. **Fallback:**
    - If driver declines, next driver tried
    - If all decline, expand search area
    - If still no driver, notify customer

7. **Monitoring:**
    - Operations monitors assignment success
    - Identifies areas with driver shortage
    - Takes action to recruit drivers

**Alternative Paths:**

- **Batch Dispatch:** Multiple orders to one driver
- **Pre-Dispatch:** Assign driver before order ready
- **Manual Dispatch:** Operations manually assigns
- **Zone Dispatch:** Driver covers specific zone

**Success Criteria:**

- Assignment rate > 95%
- Acceptance rate > 80%
- Assignment time < 30 seconds
- Driver travel time < 5 minutes

**Metrics Tracked:**

- Assignment success rate
- Driver acceptance rate
- Average assignment time
- Driver utilization

**Failure Scenarios and Handling:**

| Failure Scenario     | Expected Handling               |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| No drivers available | Expand area, recruit urgently   |
| Low acceptance rate  | Adjust pay, investigate causes  |
| System failure       | Manual dispatch, backup system  |
| Driver no-show       | Reassign, customer compensation |

## 7. Domain Rules and Invariants

### INV01: Order Status State Machine

- **Rule:** Orders must follow defined status transitions
- **Rationale:** Process integrity and tracking
- **Enforcement:** State machine validation
- **Exception:** Admin override for support

### INV02: Payment Before Order

- **Rule:** Payment must be confirmed before order sent to restaurant
- **Rationale:** Prevent unpaid orders
- **Enforcement:** Payment validation before order creation
- **Exception:** Account credit for enterprise customers

### INV03: Driver Assignment Validation

- **Rule:** Driver must be available and within service area
- **Rationale:** Ensure feasible delivery
- **Enforcement:** Driver availability check
- **Exception:** Emergency dispatch with customer consent

### INV04: Restaurant Operating Hours

- **Rule:** Orders only accepted during restaurant hours
- **Rationale:** Prevent invalid orders
- **Enforcement:** Hour validation on order placement
- **Exception:** Scheduled orders for future open hours

### INV05: Delivery Time Minimum

- **Rule:** Minimum delivery time cannot be less than preparation time
- **Rationale:** Realistic expectations
- **Enforcement:** ETA calculation includes prep time
- **Exception:** Pickup orders

### INV06: Driver Rating Threshold

- **Rule:** Drivers below rating threshold cannot accept orders
- **Rationale:** Maintain service quality
- **Enforcement:** Rating check before order offer
- **Exception:** Retraining period with supervision

### INV07: Restaurant Rating Display

- **Rule:** Restaurant ratings must be accurate and verifiable
- **Rationale:** Customer trust
- **Enforcement:** Rating calculation from verified orders
- **Exception:** New restaurants (no rating displayed)

### INV08: Payment Idempotency

- **Rule:** Duplicate payment requests must not charge twice
- **Rationale:** Prevent double charging
- **Enforcement:** Idempotency keys on payment API
- **Exception:** None

### INV09: Tip Non-Refundable

- **Rule:** Tips cannot be refunded to platform
- **Rationale:** Tips belong to drivers
- **Enforcement:** Refund calculation excludes tips
- **Exception:** Driver-initiated return with approval

### INV10: Order Modification Window

- **Rule:** Orders can only be modified before preparation starts
- **Rationale:** Prevent kitchen disruption
- **Enforcement:** Modification validation
- **Exception:** Support override with restaurant approval

### INV11: Menu Price Non-Negative

- **Rule:** Menu item prices cannot be negative
- **Rationale:** Prevent pricing errors and fraud
- **Enforcement:** Price validation on create/update
- **Exception:** None

### INV12: Restaurant Menu Consistency

- **Rule:** Menu changes cannot affect active orders
- **Rationale:** Order integrity
- **Enforcement:** Order items snapshotted at order time
- **Exception:** None
