### Problem Statement

Retail banks face critical challenges in an increasingly competitive and regulated financial services environment:

1. **Digital Transformation Imperative:** Customer expectations have fundamentally shifted toward digital-first banking experiences. 70%+ of customers expect seamless mobile and online banking capabilities comparable to fintech competitors. Legacy systems built decades ago cannot deliver modern UX, real-time processing, or API-enabled ecosystems. Banks risk customer attrition, particularly among younger demographics (millennials, Gen Z) who prefer digital engagement and expect instant gratification in banking services.

2. **Legacy System Modernization:** Most traditional banks operate on mainframe-based core banking systems from the 1970s-1990s, creating technical debt that impedes innovation. These monolithic architectures are expensive to maintain, difficult to integrate with modern services, and cannot scale efficiently with cloud technologies. Banks must modernize while maintaining 24/7 operations, ensuring data integrity, and managing complex regulatory requirements—all without service disruption.

3. **Customer Acquisition and Retention:** Banks compete not only with traditional rivals but also with neobanks (Chime, Revolut, N26), BigTech (Apple Card, Google Pay), and fintech disruptors (SoFi, Affirm). Customer acquisition costs have increased 40%+ over the past decade while lifetime values decrease as customers hold fewer accounts per institution. Differentiation through personalized experiences, competitive rates, and innovative products becomes critical.

4. **Regulatory Compliance Burden:** Banking remains one of the most heavily regulated industries. Requirements span federal, state, and international jurisdictions including Bank Secrecy Act, USA PATRIOT Act, Dodd-Frank, Consumer Financial Protection Bureau rules, state usury laws, GDPR, PSD2/PSD3, and numerous other regulations. Compliance failures result in billions in fines annually (JPMorgan $2B+ in 2023, Citigroup $1.8B). Banks need embedded compliance that doesn't impede customer experience.

5. **Fraud Prevention and Security:** Financial fraud costs the industry $40B+ annually in the US alone, with losses increasing as transactions move digital. Card-not-present fraud, account takeover, identity theft, synthetic identity fraud, and authorized push payment fraud require sophisticated, real-time detection capabilities. Banks must protect customers while minimizing false positives that degrade user experience.

6. **Operating Margin Pressure:** Net interest margins face headwinds from interest rate volatility and competitive deposit pricing. Non-interest income becomes critical, requiring cross-sell capabilities and fee optimization. Operational efficiency through automation and digital channels becomes essential as cost-to-income ratios face investor scrutiny.

7. **Personalization at Scale:** Modern customers expect personalized product recommendations, financial insights, and proactive service. Legacy systems lack the data models and real-time processing capabilities needed for hyper-personalization. Banks need to leverage transaction data, behavioral analytics, and AI/ML to deliver relevant experiences at millions of customer interactions daily.

### Solution Overview

BankCore provides a comprehensive, cloud-native platform for modern retail banking operations:

- **Account Management:** Complete lifecycle management for checking, savings, money market, CDs, and brokerage accounts with real-time balance updates, hold management, and multi-currency support
- **Digital Banking Experience:** Progressive web applications, iOS and Android mobile apps, and open APIs for third-party integrations (plaid-like connectivity, open banking compliance)
- **Payment Processing Hub:** ACH, wires, real-time payments (FedNow, RTP, SEPA Instant, UPI), card processing, bill payments, peer-to-peer transfers, and cross-border remittances
- **Lending Platform:** Personal loans, auto loans, mortgages, HELOCs, and credit cards with complete origination, underwriting, funding, and servicing workflows
- **Fraud Management:** AI-powered real-time transaction scoring, behavioral biometrics, device fingerprinting, network analysis, and automated investigation workflows
- **Compliance Engine:** Embedded KYC, AML monitoring, sanctions screening, regulatory reporting, and audit trails across all customer interactions and transactions
- **Customer Intelligence:** Transaction categorization, spending insights, budgeting tools, savings goals, credit score monitoring, and personalized product recommendations
- **Integration Ecosystem:** Pre-built connectors for credit bureaus, identity verification, document imaging, payment networks, core ledgers, and business intelligence platforms

### KPI Calculation Methodology

**Digital Adoption Rate:** (Mobile transactions + Online banking transactions + API transactions) / Total transactions × 100, calculated daily with breakdown by transaction type.

**Customer Acquisition Cost:** (Marketing spend + Sales compensation + Onboarding costs) / Number of new verified accounts, calculated monthly by acquisition channel.

**Fraud Loss Ratio:** (Confirmed fraud losses + Chargebacks) / Total transaction volume × 100,000 (expressed as basis points), calculated daily with attribution to fraud type.

**Loan-to-Deposit Ratio:** Total loan portfolio balance / Total deposit balance × 100, calculated daily for liquidity management.

**Non-Performing Loans:** (Loans 90+ days past due + Accrued loans on non-accrual status) / Total loans × 100, calculated monthly for regulatory reporting.

## 4. Core User Journeys (10+)

### J01: Digital Account Opening (New Checking Account)

**Scenario:** First-time customer opens a checking account entirely online

**Stages:**

1. **Landing and Selection:** Customer navigates to bank website or app, selects "Open Account," chooses checking account type
2. **Eligibility Check:** System verifies customer location, age (18+), and basic eligibility
3. **Application Form:** Customer provides personal information (name, DOB, SSN, address, employment)
4. **Identity Verification:** Third-party verification (SSN validation, address verification, potentially document upload or video KYC)
5. **Credit Check:** Soft pull credit check for fraud detection and risk scoring (disclosed and consented)
6. **Account Selection:** Customer chooses account features, linked savings, debit card options
7. **Initial Funding:** Customer funds account via external transfer, card, or pending deposit
8. **Agreements:** Customer e-signs account agreements, disclosures, and regulatory notices
9. **Verification:** Customer receives code via text/call to verify phone ownership
10. **Completion:** Account activated, debit card ordered, online banking credentials set

**Success Criteria:**

- Time to completion: <15 minutes
- Abandonment rate: <30%
- First-time approval rate: >80%
- Account activation within 24 hours: >95%

**Edge Cases:**

- Failed identity verification → Manual review pathway
- Credit check fraud indicators → Enhanced due diligence
- Initial funding fails → Alternative funding options
- Address verification fail → Document upload option

### J02: Mobile Check Deposit

**Scenario:** Customer deposits a paper check using mobile app

**Stares:**

1. **Initiation:** Customer logs into mobile app, selects "Deposit Check"
2. **Account Selection:** Customer chooses destination account
3. **Check Capture:** Customer photographs front and back of check with guided framing
4. **Amount Entry:** Customer enters check amount (OCR validates)
5. **Image Quality Check:** System validates image clarity, all corners visible, no glare
6. **Endorsement:** Customer confirms check is endorsed or uses digital endorsement
7. **Review and Submit:** Customer reviews deposit details and confirms
8. **Processing:** System processes images, performs fraud checks, posts deposit
9. **Confirmation:** Customer receives confirmation with availability schedule
10. **Check Retention:** Customer retains check for 14 days per Regulation CC

**Success Criteria:**

- Image capture success rate: >95%
- Average deposit time: <3 minutes
- First-time approval rate: >90%
- Return rate: <2%

**Edge Cases:**

- Poor image quality → Retake with guidance
- Check amount mismatch → Customer confirmation required
- Duplicate deposit detection → Block with explanation
- Exceeds deposit limits → Approval workflow

### J03: Bill Payment Setup and Execution

**Scenario:** Customer sets up recurring bill payment to utility company

**Stages:**

1. **Navigation:** Customer opens app, selects "Pay Bills" or "Bill Pay"
2. **Add Payee:** Customer searches for payee or enters manually (name, address, account number)
3. **Payee Verification:** System validates payee information against billing database
4. **Payment Type:** Customer selects one-time or recurring (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly)
5. **Payment Details:** Customer enters amount, payment date, memo/description
6. **Source Account:** Customer selects account to fund payment
7. **Review:** Customer reviews payment schedule and details
8. **Confirmation:** Customer confirms and submits payment
9. **Scheduling:** System schedules payment in payment queue
10. **Processing:** On payment date, system debits account and sends payment
11. **Confirmation:** Customer receives notification of successful payment

**Success Criteria:**

- Payee setup time: <3 minutes
- Payment success rate: >98%
- On-time payment rate: >99%
- Failed payment notification: <1 hour

**Edge Cases:**

- Insufficient funds → Payment delay or rejection with notification
- Invalid payee → Correction workflow or manual processing
- Recurring payment decline → Stop sequence and customer alert
- Payment date on holiday → Process on next business day

## 7. Domain Rules and Invariants (10+)

### INV01: Account Balance Cannot Go Negative (Unless Overdraft Protection)

**Rule:** Debit transactions must be validated against available balance before posting, unless overdraft protection is enabled and configured.

**Implementation:**

- Check available balance before each debit transaction
- Available balance = Current balance - Holds - Pending debits
- If insufficient funds:
    - If overdraft protection enabled: Process with overdraft fee/transfer
    - If overdraft opt-in: Decline transaction with "insufficient funds"
    - If check/ACH: Process with NSF fee (if relationship overdraft)
- Maintain running balance with transaction-level accuracy
- Balance accuracy to penny (2 decimal places)

**Violation Handling:**

- Transaction declined with clear error message
- Customer notified of insufficient funds
- No negative balance without explicit overdraft relationship

### CC07A: CCARD Act (Credit Card Accountability Responsibility and Disclosure)

**Regulation:** 15 USC 1637 et seq., 12 CFR Part 1026

**Purpose:** Protect credit card consumers through fair billing, fee transparency, and payment allocation rules.

**Requirements:**

- **Billing Error Resolution (Regulation Z, CCARD amendments):**

    - Cannot report disputed amount as delinquent during investigation

    - Minimum payment warning showing time to pay off balance

- **Payment Due Date:**
    - Due date at least 21 days after statement closing date
    - Fixed due date each month
    - Grace period for purchases if balance paid in full

- **Payment Allocation Hierarchy:**

    - Then to lower APR balances in descending order
    - Clear disclosure of allocation methodology
    - Option to request different allocation (subject to terms)

- **Overdraft Protection Hierarchy:**
    - Clear disclosure of overdraft protection options
    - Hierarchy for multiple linked accounts:
        1. Savings account (if linked and sufficient balance)
        2. Line of credit (if approved)
        3. Another debit card account
    - Customer must opt-in for debit card overdraft coverage
    - Maximum $35 overdraft fee per transaction (industry standard)
    - Overdraft fee cap per day (typically 2-3 occurrences)

- **Payment Time and Credit:**
    - Payment credited if received by 5 PM local time on due date
    - If received after 5 PM, credited next business day
    - Must honor stop-payment requests
    - Clear payment receipt and processing timestamps

**Implementation:**

### CC07B: Truth in Savings (Regulation DD)

**Regulation:** 12 CFR Part 1030

**Purpose:** Ensure clear and accurate disclosure of deposit account terms, rates, and fees.

**Requirements:**

- **APY (Annual Percentage Yield) Disclosure:**
    - APY must be prominently disclosed (larger font than APR)
    - APY calculation must follow federal formula
    - APY must be available for first 60 days
    - Variable rate accounts: current APY and explanation of variability

- **Interest Rate Disclosure:**
    - Periodic rate (interest rate) separate from APY
    - Compounding frequency disclosure
    - Daily balance vs. average daily balance method
    - Minimum balance requirements for interest earnings

- **APY Disclosure Timing:**
    - At account opening
    - Before rate changes (21 days notice for decreases)
    - At time of passbook update or statement
    - In advertisements when rates mentioned

- **Fee Disclosure:**
    - All fees must be disclosed in account agreement
    - Monthly maintenance fees
    - Transaction fees
    - Minimum balance fees
    - ATM fees and surcharge policies

- **Statement Requirements:**
    - Periodic statements (quarterly minimum)
    - Interest earned during period
    - Fees charged during period
    - APY used for calculations
    - Balance computation method

**Day-Count Convention Requirements:**

- **Actual/365 (Most Common):**
    - Interest = Principal × Rate × (Days/365)
    - Used for most consumer deposit accounts
    - Leap year handling: 366 days in February
    - Most favorable to consumers

- **30/360:**
    - Interest = Principal × Rate × (Days/360)
    - Used for some savings accounts and CDs
    - Each month treated as 30 days
    - Year always 360 days
    - Can be more or less favorable depending on compounding

- **Actual/360:**
    - Interest = Principal × Rate × (Days/360)
    - More favorable to banks
    - Less common for consumer accounts
    - Must be clearly disclosed

- **Implementation Requirements:**
    - Day-count convention clearly disclosed in account agreement
    - Consistent application across account type
    - Accurate calculation with daily compounding
    - Leap year handling documented
    - Leap second handling (negligible but documented)

**Implementation:**

- APY calculation engine with federal formula
- Day-count convention configuration (Actual/365, 30/360, Actual/360)
- Interest accrual with daily compounding
- Statement generation with interest and fee breakdown
- Rate change notification workflow
- Advertisement review for rate disclosures

**Evidence:**

## 10. Failure Scenarios and Resilience (10+)

### FSC01: Database Failover

**Scenario:** Primary database becomes unavailable due to hardware failure, network issue, or regional outage.

**Impact:** All systems dependent on database become unavailable.

**Recovery:**

- Automatic failover to standby database
- Data replication ensures minimal data loss
- RTO: <5 minutes
- RPO: <1 minute (with synchronous replication)

**Mitigation:**

- Active-passive or active-active database architecture
- Continuous replication
- Regular failover testing
- Multi-region deployment

---

### FSC02: Payment Network Outage

**Scenario:** Visa/Mastercard or ACH network experiences outage.

**Impact:** Card transactions or ACH payments cannot be processed.

**Recovery:**

- Queue transactions for retry
- Customer notification of delays
- Alternative payment methods offered

**Mitigation:**

- Multiple network relationships
- Transaction queuing
- Timeout and retry logic
- Customer communication templates

---

### FSC03: Third-Party API Failure

**Scenario:** External service (credit bureau, identity verification, etc.) becomes unavailable.

**Impact:** Dependent workflows blocked (account opening, loan origination).

**Recovery:**

- Circuit breaker activation
- Manual process activation
- Queue and retry when service recovers

**Mitigation:**

- Redundant third-party providers
- Graceful degradation
- Manual override capability
- Service level monitoring

---

### FSC04: Fraud System False Positive Surge

**Scenario:** Fraud detection rules trigger excessive false positives, blocking legitimate transactions.

**Impact:** Customer experience degradation, increased support volume.

**Recovery:**

- Rule tuning and adjustment
- Manual review acceleration
- Customer communication

**Mitigation:**

- Continuous model monitoring
- A/B testing for rule changes
- Fast rule deployment capability
- Customer experience metrics monitoring

---

### FSC05: Data Corruption

- Restore from backup

- Manual reconciliation

**Mitigation:**

- Regular integrity checks
- Backup verification
- Transaction log preservation
- Multi-region data replication

## 12. Security and Access Control

### Authentication

- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) required for all customers
- Support for SMS, authenticator app, hardware token, biometric
- Password complexity requirements (12+ characters, mixed case, numbers, special chars)
- Password history (cannot reuse last 12 passwords)
- Account lockout after 5 failed attempts
- Session timeout after 15 minutes inactivity
- Step-up authentication for sensitive transactions (wires, large transfers)
- Single sign-on (SSO) for internal systems

### Authorization

- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) for employees
- Customer-level data isolation (employees cannot see all customers)
- Need-to-know principle
- Customer consent for data sharing
- Granular permissions for internal roles

### Roles and Permissions

**Customer Roles:**

- Account Owner: Full access to owned accounts
- Joint Owner: Full access to joint accounts
- Authorized User: Limited access (view, transfer, no close)
- Beneficiary: Post-death access only

**Employee Roles:**

- Teller: Transaction processing, limited account access
- Customer Service: Account inquiries, basic changes
- Relationship Manager: Full customer access for assigned portfolio
- Loan Officer: Loan application and origination
- Compliance Officer: Full access for compliance purposes
- Fraud Analyst: Fraud investigation access
- System Administrator: System configuration (no customer data)

### Encryption

- Data at rest: AES-256 encryption
- Data in transit: TLS 1.3
- Card data: Tokenization (PCI-DSS compliant)
- Sensitive fields: Field-level encryption (SSN, phone, address)
- Key management: Hardware Security Module (HSM)
- Key rotation: Annual minimum

### Audit Trail

- All data access logged
- All data modifications logged (before/after)
- All authentication events logged
- All administrative actions logged
- Log retention: 7 years
- Immutable logs (write-once)
- Audit log integrity verification
