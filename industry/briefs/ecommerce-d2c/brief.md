# E-commerce D2C Platform - Universal-Fully Brief

## 1. Product Context

### Product Name

ShopDirect Pro - Enterprise D2C E-commerce Platform

### Product Type

B2C Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) E-commerce Platform with Multi-Channel Sales Capabilities

### Target Market

- **Primary Markets:** North America (US, Canada), Western Europe (UK, Germany, France), APAC (Australia, Japan, South Korea)
- **Customer Segments:** Mid-market to enterprise brands ($10M-$500M annual revenue) selling physical products directly to consumers
- **Product Categories:** Fashion & Apparel, Home & Living, Beauty & Personal Care, Electronics, Sports & Outdoors

### Problem Statement

Modern brands face critical challenges when building direct-to-consumer sales channels:

1. **Fragmented Commerce Infrastructure:** Brands must integrate 10+ disparate systems (catalog, cart, checkout, payments, shipping, CRM, analytics, inventory, promotions, customer service) leading to data silos, inconsistent experiences, and operational inefficiencies.

2. **Slow Time-to-Market:** Traditional e-commerce platforms require 6-18 months for initial launch with custom development. Market opportunities are lost while competitors capture customer mindshare.

3. **Poor Customer Experience:** Non-personalized, slow-loading, non-responsive sites result in 70%+ cart abandonment rates. Inconsistent experience across web, mobile, and emerging channels frustrates customers.

4. **Inventory and Fulfillment Complexity:** Brands struggle to maintain accurate inventory across multiple warehouses, handle backorders intelligently, optimize shipping costs, and provide reliable delivery estimates.

5. **Compliance Burden:** E-commerce brands must navigate GDPR, CCPA, PCI-DSS, tax compliance across jurisdictions, and industry-specific regulations while maintaining audit-ready systems.

6. **Limited Business Intelligence:** Disconnected analytics prevent data-driven decisions. Brands cannot accurately measure customer lifetime value, attribution across channels, or optimize marketing spend effectively.

### Solution Overview

ShopDirect Pro is a comprehensive D2C e-commerce platform that provides:

- **Unified Commerce Engine:** Single platform handling catalog management, shopping cart, checkout, order processing, and fulfillment orchestration
- **Omnichannel Experience:** Consistent branded experience across web, mobile web, Progressive Web App (PWA), and social commerce integrations
- **Intelligent Personalization:** AI-driven product recommendations, search optimization, and dynamic content based on user behavior and preferences
- **Flexible Fulfillment:** Multi-warehouse inventory management, intelligent order routing, carrier selection, and real-time shipping calculations
- **Enterprise Integrations:** Pre-built connectors for ERP (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite), CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot), payment gateways (Stripe, Adyen, PayPal), and marketing platforms
- **Compliance-First Architecture:** Built-in GDPR, CCPA, PCI-DSS compliance with audit trails, data residency controls, and automated tax calculation
- **Real-Time Analytics:** Embedded business intelligence with custom dashboarding, conversion funnel analysis, and customer journey tracking

### Business Model

- **Revenue Model:** SaaS subscription with tiered pricing based on GMV (Gross Merchandise Value)
    - Starter: $999/month for up to $500K annual GMV
    - Growth: $2,499/month for up to $5M annual GMV
    - Enterprise: $4,999/month for up to $25M annual GMV
    - Custom: Volume-based pricing for $25M+ annual GMV

- **Transaction Fees:** 0.5% on processed transactions (waived for Enterprise tier with $100K+ monthly commitment)

- **Implementation Services:** Professional services for migration, customization, and integration ($25K-$250K one-time)

- **App Marketplace:** 15% commission on third-party app subscriptions sold through platform marketplace

### Go-to-Market Strategy

- **Launch Phase (Months 1-6):** Direct sales to 50 design partners in North America with 40% discount for feedback and case studies
- **Growth Phase (Months 7-18):** Partner channel development with 20 implementation partners; inbound marketing focused on SEO and content; attendance at 10+ industry events
- **Expansion Phase (Months 19-36):** International expansion to Europe and APAC; acquisition of complementary apps; enterprise account team for $10M+ revenue brands

## 4. Core User Journeys

### J01: Merchant Onboarding and Store Setup

**Primary Persona:** P01 (Elena Martinez - E-commerce Manager)

**Trigger Event:** Merchant signs up for platform subscription

**Preconditions:**

- Merchant has valid business email and payment method
- Merchant has domain name ready or will use platform subdomain
- Merchant has banking information for payouts

**Postconditions:**

- Store is live and accessible
- Payment gateway is configured and test transaction successful
- At least one product category and product are created
- Merchant can access admin dashboard

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. Merchant completes signup form with business information
2. Merchant verifies email address via confirmation link
3. Merchant completes guided onboarding wizard:
    - Step 1: Store basics (name, domain, currency, timezone)
    - Step 2: Payment gateway selection and configuration
    - Step 3: Shipping zones and rates setup
    - Step 4: Tax settings (auto-calculate or manual)
4. Merchant installs base theme from theme gallery
5. Merchant customizes store branding (logo, colors, fonts)
6. Merchant creates first product category
7. Merchant adds first product with images and pricing
8. Merchant configures legal pages (privacy, terms, returns)
9. Merchant runs checkout test with test payment
10. Merchant clicks "Launch Store" to go live

**Alternative Paths:**

- **Import from existing platform:** Skip steps 4-7, import products/categories from CSV or another platform
- **Use agency onboarding:** Implementation partner completes steps 3-9 on behalf of merchant
- **Trial conversion:** Steps 1-2 include trial signup flow with credit card collection

**Success Criteria:**

- Store is publicly accessible within 60 minutes of signup
- Test transaction completes successfully
- First product is viewable on storefront

**Metrics Tracked:**

- Time from signup to store launch
- Onboarding completion rate (percentage completing all steps)
- Step-by-step drop-off rates

### J02: Product Creation and Management

**Primary Persona:** P01 (E-commerce Manager)

**Trigger Event:** New product needs to be added to catalog

**Preconditions:**

- Merchant is authenticated and has Edit Products permission
- Product category exists or will be created
- Product images and media are prepared

**Postconditions:**

- Product is live and visible on storefront
- Product appears in search results
- Product inventory is tracked
- Product can be added to cart

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. Merchant navigates to Products > All Products > Add Product
2. Merchant enters basic product information:
    - Product title and description
    - Product type and vendor
    - Unique product identifier (SKU, barcode)
3. Merchant sets pricing:
    - Base price
    - Compare-at price (for sale items)
    - Cost per item (for margin tracking)
    - Tax settings
4. Merchant configures inventory:
    - Inventory tracking enabled
    - Initial inventory quantity
    - Allow backorders (yes/no)
    - Low stock threshold
5. Merchant adds product media:
    - Upload primary and gallery images
    - Add product videos (optional)
    - Set image alt text for accessibility
6. Merchant configures product variants (if applicable):
    - Create variant options (size, color, material)
    - Set price and inventory per variant
    - Add variant-specific images
7. Merchant assigns to collections/categories
8. Merchant configures SEO (meta title, description, URL handle)
9. Merchant saves and publishes product

**Alternative Paths:**

- **Draft mode:** Product saved as draft for subsequent publishing
- **Bulk import:** Products imported via CSV instead of individual creation
- **Scheduled publish:** Product scheduled to go live at specific date/time

**Success Criteria:**

- Product appears in storefront within 1 minute of publishing
- Product is searchable by title, SKU, and description
- Product can be added to cart with correct pricing

**Metrics Tracked:**

- Time to create first product
- Number of products per merchant
- Draft-to-published conversion rate

### J04: Shopping Cart and Checkout

**Primary Persona:** External Customer (end shopper)

**Trigger Event:** Customer decides to purchase items

**Preconditions:**

- Customer has items in shopping cart
- Customer has valid shipping address
- Items are in stock and available for purchase

**Postconditions:**

- Order is confirmed and payment is processed
- Customer receives order confirmation
- Order appears in merchant's order management

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. Customer clicks "Checkout" from cart page
2. Customer enters email address (for guest or account lookup)
3. Customer enters shipping information:
    - Full name
    - Address
    - City, state, ZIP/postal code
    - Phone number
4. Customer selects shipping method:
    - Standard, expedited, or overnight
    - Pickup in-store (if available)
5. Customer reviews order:
    - Items and quantities
    - Subtotal, taxes, shipping
    - Total amount
6. Customer enters payment information:
    - Credit card details or selects saved payment
    - Or selects alternative payment (PayPal, Apple Pay, etc.)
7. Customer applies discount code (if applicable)
8. Customer reviews final order summary
9. Customer clicks "Place Order"
10. Payment is processed (redirect or inline depending on gateway)
11. Customer sees order confirmation page with order number
12. Customer receives order confirmation email

**Alternative Paths:**

- **Account checkout:** Logged-in customer auto-fills address and payment
- **Express checkout:** Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal express flow
- **Buy Now button:** Skip cart, direct to checkout from product page
- **Abandonment:** Customer leaves during checkout, receives recovery email

**Success Criteria:**

- Checkout completes within 3 minutes for returning customer
- Payment success rate > 95%
- No checkout errors for valid input

**Metrics Tracked:**

- Checkout conversion rate
- Checkout abandonment rate by step
- Payment method distribution
- Checkout completion time

## 7. Domain Rules and Invariants

### INV01: Order Total Integrity

- **Name:** Order Total Must Equal Sum of Line Items Plus Fees
- **Description:** The total amount of an order must always equal the sum of line item prices plus taxes, shipping, and discounts
- **Formal Statement:** `order.total = sum(order.line_items.price * quantity) + order.tax + order.shipping - order.discounts`
- **Enforcement Point:** Compile-time (schema validation) + Runtime (before order creation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `ORDER_TOTAL_MISMATCH`; reject order creation
- **Test Cases:** Order with mismatched total; order after discount change; order with tax change

### INV02: Inventory Non-Negative

- **Name:** Available Inventory Cannot Go Negative
- **Description:** The available inventory for any product variant cannot be less than zero unless backorders are explicitly enabled
- **Formal Statement:** `∀p ∈ Products, ∀v ∈ Variants, (v.backorder_enabled OR v.available_quantity >= 0)`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (before order confirmation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `INSUFFICIENT_INVENTORY`; show out-of-stock message
- **Test Cases:** Order exceeding stock; concurrent orders for last item; inventory adjustment to negative

### INV03: Order Status State Machine

- **Name:** Order Status Must Follow Valid State Transitions
- **Description:** Order status must only transition through predefined valid paths
- **Formal Statement:** Valid transitions: `PENDING → CONFIRMED → PAID → FULFILLED → COMPLETED` with allowed bypasses and reversals
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on status update)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `INVALID_STATUS_TRANSITION`; reject transition
- **Test Cases:** Invalid transition attempt; concurrent status updates; rollback after fulfillment

### INV04: Refund Cannot Exceed Paid Amount

- **Name:** Total Refunds Cannot Exceed Original Order Total
- **Description:** The sum of all refunds for an order cannot exceed the original paid amount
- **Formal Statement:** `∀o ∈ Orders, sum(o.refunds.amount) <= o.total_paid`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (before refund creation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `OVER_REFUND`; reject refund
- **Test Cases:** Multiple partial refunds exceeding total; refund after partial refund; currency change scenario

### INV05: Customer Email Uniqueness

- **Name:** Customer Email Must Be Unique
- **Description:** No two customer accounts can have the same normalized email address
- **Formal Statement:** `∀c1, c2 ∈ Customers, c1.id != c2.id → normalize(c1.email) != normalize(c2.email)`
- **Enforcement Point:** Compile-time (unique index) + Runtime (before creation/update)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `EMAIL_ALREADY_EXISTS`; prompt for different email or login
- **Test Cases:** Duplicate registration; case variation (Test@Example.com); email change to existing email

### INV06: Discount Application Rules

- **Name:** Discount Must Meet Eligibility Criteria
- **Description:** A discount can only be applied if all eligibility conditions are met
- **Formal Statement:** `discount.applicable = discount.valid_dates AND discount.usage_limit AND discount.customer_eligible AND discount.product_eligible AND discount.order_minimum`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (at checkout)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `DISCOUNT_NOT_APPLICABLE`; show reason for ineligibility
- **Test Cases:** Expired discount; usage limit reached; customer not in group; order below minimum

### INV07: Payment Idempotency

- **Name:** Duplicate Payment Requests Must Not Charge Twice
- **Description:** Payment processing must be idempotent to prevent duplicate charges
- **Formal Statement:** `payment.charge(idempotency_key) → unique_charge_per_key`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (payment gateway integration)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `DUPLICATE_REQUEST`; return original charge result
- **Test Cases:** Retry with same key; network timeout retry; concurrent payment attempts

### INV08: Shipping Address Validation

- **Name:** Shipping Address Must Be Deliverable
- **Description:** Shipping addresses must pass validation before order confirmation
- **Formal Statement:** `order.shipping_address.valid = address_service.validate(order.shipping_address)`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (at checkout)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `INVALID_SHIPPING_ADDRESS`; show correction suggestions
- **Test Cases:** Invalid ZIP code; incomplete address; PO box without carrier support

### INV09: Tenant Data Isolation

- **Name:** Merchant Data Must Be Isolated
- **Description:** Data from one merchant tenant must never be accessible by another merchant
- **Formal Statement:** `∀m1, m2 ∈ Merchants, m1.id != m2.id → ∀d ∈ m1.data, d.tenant_id = m1.id`
- **Enforcement Point:** Compile-time (tenant context) + Runtime (all queries)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `TENANT_ISOLATION_VIOLATION`; log security incident
- **Test Cases:** Cross-tenant query attempt; API request with wrong tenant context; export with tenant filter

### INV10: Audit Log Immutability

- **Name:** Audit Log Entries Cannot Be Modified or Deleted
- **Description:** Once created, audit log entries must be immutable
- **Formal Statement:** `∀a ∈ AuditLog, created(a) → immutable(a)`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on update/delete attempt)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `AUDIT_MODIFICATION_DENIED`; log attempt as security event
- **Test Cases:** Direct database modification attempt; API delete request; admin override attempt

### INV11: Product Price Non-Negative

- **Name:** Product Prices Cannot Be Negative
- **Description:** Product prices and discounts cannot result in negative final price
- **Formal Statement:** `∀p ∈ Products, p.final_price >= 0`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on price update, at checkout)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `INVALID_PRICE`; cap discount at product price
- **Test Cases:** Negative price entry; discount exceeding price; promotion stacking to negative

### INV12: Order Currency Consistency

- **Name:** Order Line Items Must Use Same Currency
- **Description:** All line items in an order must use the same currency as the order
- **Formal Statement:** `∀o ∈ Orders, ∀li ∈ o.line_items, li.currency = o.currency`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on cart/checkout)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `CURRENCY_MISMATCH`; convert or remove incompatible items
- **Test Cases:** Multi-currency cart; currency change with items; promotion in different currency

## 11. Security and Access Control

### Authentication Strategy

- **Primary Mechanism:** JWT (JSON Web Tokens) with RS256 signature
- **Token Lifetimes:**
    - Access token: 15 minutes
    - Refresh token: 7 days (rotating)
- **OAuth 2.0 Support:** Authorization Code Flow for third-party apps
- **SSO Support:** SAML 2.0 for enterprise customers
- **Social Login:** OAuth providers (Google, Facebook)
- **Password Requirements:**
    - Minimum 12 characters
    - Must include uppercase, lowercase, number, special character
    - Cannot be in common password list (HaveIBeenPwned API check)
    - Password history: last 10 passwords

### Authorization Model

**Type:** Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with Attribute-Based Access Control (ABAC) for fine-grained permissions

**Role Hierarchy:**

1. Super Admin (platform)
2. Tenant Admin (merchant owner)
3. E-commerce Manager
4. Marketing Manager
5. Developer
6. Customer Service
7. Finance
8. Content Editor
9. Viewer

### Session Management

- **Timeout:** 30 minutes of inactivity
- **Maximum Sessions:** 10 concurrent sessions per user
- **Session Storage:** Redis with 15-minute TTL (access), 7-day TTL (refresh)
- **Invalidation:** On password change, explicit logout, admin action
- **Remember Me:** 30-day extended session option

### API Security

- **Rate Limiting:**
    - Public API: 100 requests/minute per API key
    - Admin API: 500 requests/minute per user
    - Bulk API: 10 requests/minute per tenant
- **Input Validation:** All inputs validated and sanitized (OWASP guidelines)
- **CORS:** Configurable per tenant; default: same-origin only
- **HTTPS:** Required for all API requests
- **IP Whitelisting:** Optional for enterprise tenants

### Data Protection

- **Encryption at Rest:** AES-256 for all databases
- **Encryption in Transit:** TLS 1.3 for all connections
- **Key Management:** AWS KMS / HashiCorp Vault
- **Key Rotation:** Every 90 days
- **Sensitive Fields:** Encrypted separately (PII, payment tokens)

### Secret Management

- **Platform Secrets:** HashiCorp Vault
- **Tenant Secrets:** Encrypted in database with tenant-specific keys
- **API Keys:** Rotatable; separate keys per environment
- **Webhook Secrets:** Per-webhook HMAC signing

### Audit Logging

**Logged Events:**

- Authentication (login, logout, password change, 2FA)
- Authorization (permission denied)
- Data modification (create, update, delete)
- Configuration changes
- API key usage
- Export actions
- Sensitive data access

**Log Retention:** 10 years for financial; 7 years for PII-related; immutable storage

## 15. Open Questions

### OQ01: Data Residency for EU Customers

- **Question:** Should EU merchant data be stored exclusively in EU regions, or can we use multi-region replication?
- **Impact:** High (affects infrastructure costs and architecture)
- **Decision Deadline:** 2026-05-15
- **Dependencies:** GDPR compliance strategy
- **Proposed Options:**
    - Option A: Strict EU residency for EU tenants (higher cost)
    - Option B: Primary region with automatic failover to other regions (lower cost, requires SCCL)
- **Decision Owner:** CTO / Legal

### OQ02: Self-Service vs. Assisted Onboarding

- **Question:** Should the MVP support fully self-service onboarding or require assisted setup for enterprise?
- **Impact:** Medium (affects implementation team size)
- **Decision Deadline:** 2026-04-30
- **Dependencies:** Customer success resource planning
- **Proposed Options:**
    - Option A: Self-service for all, optional assisted add-on
    - Option B: Tiered approach (self-service for Starter/Growth, assisted for Enterprise)
- **Decision Owner:** VP Customer Success

### OQ03: Theme Customization Model

- **Question:** Should merchants be able to write custom code in themes, or use a visual builder only?
- **Impact:** Medium (affects developer experience and support burden)
- **Decision Deadline:** 2026-05-01
- **Dependencies:** Theme development resources
- **Proposed Options:**
    - Option A: Liquid-like templating with full code access
    - Option B: Visual builder with limited customization
    - Option C: Hybrid approach
- **Decision Owner:** VP Product

### OQ04: Tax Calculation: Build vs. Buy

- **Question:** Should we build our own tax engine or integrate with Avalara/TaxJar?
- **Impact:** High (affects development time and ongoing maintenance)
- **Decision Deadline:** 2026-04-25
- **Dependencies:** Tax compliance requirements
- **Proposed Options:**
    - Option A: Avalara integration (higher ongoing cost, faster launch)
    - Option B: TaxJar integration (middle ground)
    - Option C: Build in-house (lower cost, longer development)
- **Decision Owner:** CTO / CFO

### OQ05: Multi-Tenant Database Strategy

- **Question:** Should we use shared database with tenant_id isolation, or separate databases per tenant?
- **Impact:** High (affects scalability and security model)
- **Decision Deadline:** 2026-04-20
- **Dependencies:** Infrastructure architecture
- **Proposed Options:**
    - Option A: Shared database with row-level security
    - Option B: Schema-per-tenant
    - Option C: Database-per-tenant for Enterprise
- **Decision Owner:** CTO / Principal Engineer
