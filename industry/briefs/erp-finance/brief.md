# ERP Finance & Operations - Universal-Fully Brief

## 1. Product Context

### Product Name

FinCore Enterprise - Financial Operations and ERP Platform

### Product Type

Enterprise Resource Planning and Financial Management System

### Target Market

- **Primary Markets:** North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific
- **Customer Segments:** Mid-market to enterprise companies ($50M-$10B revenue)
- **Industries:** Manufacturing, Retail, Services, Technology, Healthcare
- **Company Sizes:** 200-10,000+ employees
- **Geographic Scope:** Single to multi-country operations

### Problem Statement

Organizations face complex financial and operational challenges:

1. **Financial Fragmentation:** Disconnected systems for accounting, budgeting, and reporting create data silos and reconciliation nightmares. Manual consolidation across subsidiaries and entities adds weeks to closing cycles.

2. **Regulatory Compliance:** Evolving accounting standards (IFRS, GAAP), tax regulations, and reporting requirements demand constant adaptation. Non-compliance risks severe penalties and reputational damage.

3. **Operational Visibility:** Limited real-time visibility into operations, inventory, procurement, and production makes decision-making reactive rather than proactive.

4. **Process Inefficiency:** Manual, repetitive processes for AP, AR, journal entries, and reconciliations consume valuable finance resources and introduce errors.

5. **Data Integrity:** Inconsistent master data across systems (vendors, customers, products) leads to reporting discrepancies and audit findings.

6. **Forecasting Accuracy:** Poor integration between operational data and financial planning results in inaccurate budgets and forecasts.

7. **Multi-Currency Complexity:** Global operations require sophisticated currency management, hedging, and consolidation across different reporting currencies.

8. **Cost Management:** Difficulty tracking costs by product, project, department, and location prevents accurate profitability analysis.

### Solution Overview

FinCore Enterprise provides a comprehensive financial and operations platform:

- **General Ledger:** Multi-entity, multi-currency, multi-GAAP accounting
- **Accounts Payable:** Invoice processing, approval workflows, payment management
- **Accounts Receivable:** Billing, collections, cash application, credit management
- **Fixed Assets:** Asset tracking, depreciation, impairments, disposals
- **Cash Management:** Banking integration, reconciliations, treasury functions
- **Budgeting and Planning:** Budget creation, forecasts, variance analysis
- **Procurement:** Purchase requisitions, approvals, vendor management
- **Inventory Management:** Stock tracking, valuations, adjustments
- **Cost Accounting:** Cost centers, cost allocation, standard costing
- **Financial Reporting:** Standard reports, custom reports, regulatory filings
- **Revenue Recognition:** ASC 606 / IFRS 15 compliant revenue booking
- **Tax Management:** Sales tax, VAT, GST, indirect tax compliance

### Business Model

- **Licensing Model:** Per-user subscription (Named and Concurrent) with module-based pricing
- **Implementation:** Professional services for configuration, data migration, integration
- **Support Tiers:** Standard, Premium, Enterprise with SLA-based support
- **Maintenance:** Annual license maintenance including updates and patches

### Competitive Landscape

- **Direct Competitors:** SAP S/4HANA, Oracle ERP Cloud, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance
- **Mid-Market Competitors:** NetSuite, Acumatica, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central
- **Differentiators:** Modern UI, faster implementation, lower total cost of ownership, industry-specific configurations
- **Market Position:** Challenger targeting organizations seeking modern alternative to legacy ERPs

### KPI Calculation Methodology

**Days to Close:** Calendar days from period end date to date when general ledger is closed and financial statements are available.

**Days Sales Outstanding (DSO):** (Accounts Receivable / Total Credit Sales) × Number of Days in Period.

**Working Capital Days:** DSO + Days Inventory Outstanding - Days Payable Outstanding.

**Inventory Turnover:** Cost of Goods Sold / Average Inventory Value.

**Forecast Accuracy:** 1 - (|Actual - Forecast| / Actual) × 100.

## 4. Core User Journeys (10+)

### J01: Invoice-to-Cash (Order to Cash)

**Actors:** Sales, AR Specialist, Customer, System

**Pre-conditions:**

- Customer exists in system with valid credit
- Products/services available
- Pricing and terms defined
- Customer credit limit approved

**Flow:**

1. Sales creates sales order from customer request
2. System checks credit limit and availability
3. Order approved (auto or manual based on rules)
4. Inventory reserved for order
5. Order picked and packed in warehouse
6. Shipping document created and goods shipped
7. System creates invoice based on shipped quantity
8. Invoice reviewed and posted to AR
9. Invoice sent to customer (email, portal, EDI)
10. Customer receives invoice and schedules payment
11. Payment received and applied to invoice
12. Cash receipt posted and bank reconciled
13. Revenue recognized per contract terms
14. Tax collected and reported

**Post-conditions:**

- Invoice posted in AR subledger
- Cash received and applied
- Revenue recognized
- GL updated
- Inventory reduced
- Cost of goods sold recorded

**Success Criteria:**

- Order to invoice cycle < 24 hours
- Invoice accuracy > 99%
- Payment received within terms
- DSO < 45 days
- Revenue recognition compliant

**Failure Scenarios:**

- Credit limit exceeded: Hold order, credit review required
- Inventory unavailable: Backorder or substitute
- Shipping error: Credit memo and re-ship
- Payment dispute: Collection workflow, potential write-off

---

### J02: Procure-to-Pay

**Actors:** Requisitioner, Buyer, Vendor, AP Specialist, System

**Pre-conditions:**

- Vendor master data approved and active
- Purchase approval hierarchy defined
- Budget available
- Catalog prices negotiated

**Flow:**

1. Employee creates purchase requisition in ERP
2. Requisition routes to approver based on amount/type
3. Approvers review and approve/reject requisition
4. Approved requisition converted to purchase order
5. Buyer reviews and sends PO to vendor
6. Vendor receives PO and ships goods/services
7. Goods received and entered in ERP (GRN)
8. Vendor invoice received (paper, EDI, electronic)
9. Invoice matched to PO and GRN (3-way match)
10. Invoice posted to AP subledger
11. Invoice approved for payment
12. Payment scheduled based on terms
13. Payment executed and cleared through bank
14. AP subledger reconciled to GL
15. Expense or asset capitalized

**Post-conditions:**

- PO created and approved
- Goods/services received
- Invoice posted and approved
- Payment executed
- GL updated
- Vendor statement balanced

**Success Criteria:**

- Procurement cycle < 5 days
- 95%+ invoices 3-way matched
- 90%+ invoices processed within SLA
- Duplicate payment rate < 0.1%
- Early payment discount capture > 80%

**Failure Scenarios:**

- PO mismatch: Exception workflow, manual review
- Vendor master incomplete: Vendor setup required
- Budget unavailable: Budget increase or requisition hold
- Payment rejected by bank: Reissue payment

## 7. Domain Rules and Invariants (10+)

### INV01: Double-Entry Accounting

**Rule:** Every journal entry must have equal debits and credits.

**Rationale:** Fundamental accounting principle; ensures ledger balance.

**Enforcement:** System validation on entry posting.

**Exception:** None.

---

### INV02: Period Close Lock

**Rule:** Closed periods cannot be modified.

**Rationale:** Audit integrity and financial statement reliability.

**Enforcement:** Period close with lock flag.

**Exception:** Reopen with CFO approval.

---

### INV03: Segregation of Duties

**Rule:** Create and approve must be different users.

**Rationale:** Internal control against fraud.

**Enforcement:** SoD rules engine.

**Exception:** None for financial transactions.

---

### INV04: Three-Way Match

**Rule:** Invoice must match PO and GRN before payment.

**Rationale:** Prevents overpayment and fraud.

**Enforcement:** Matching validation.

**Exception:** PO exception workflow.

---

### INV05: Cash Application Order

**Rule:** Apply oldest invoice first (FIFO).

**Rationale:** Accurate aging and DSO calculation.

**Enforcement:** System application logic.

**Exception:** Manual override with reason.

---

### INV06: Currency Translation

**Rule:** Foreign currency translated at period-end rate for reporting.

**Rationale:** Accounting standard compliance (IAS 21).

**Enforcement:** Translation run at close.

**Exception:** None.

---

### INV07: Revenue Recognition

**Rule:** Revenue recognized when performance obligation satisfied.

**Rationale:** ASC 606 / IFRS 15 compliance.

**Enforcement:** Revenue recognition engine.

**Exception:** None.

---

### INV08: Fixed Asset Depreciation

**Rule:** Depreciation run before period close.

**Rationale:** Accurate expense and asset value.

**Enforcement:** Close checklist enforcement.

**Exception:** Manual entry not allowed.

---

### INV09: Budget Validation

**Rule:** Budget cannot be negative (unless allowed).

**Rationale:** Invalid planning data.

**Enforcement:** Budget validation rules.

**Exception:** Configurable per account.

---

### INV10: Tax Calculation

**Rule:** Tax calculated at transaction time.

**Rationale:** Accurate liability tracking.

**Enforcement:** Tax engine on save.

**Exception:** Manual tax entry with approval.

---

### INV11: Intercompany Elimination

**Rule:** Intercompany transactions must be eliminated in consolidation.

**Rationale:** Avoid double-counting in consolidated results.

**Enforcement:** Elimination engine.

**Exception:** None.

---

### INV12: Journal Entry Approval

**Rule:** Journal entries above threshold require approval.

**Rationale:** Internal control and audit trail.

**Enforcement:** Approval workflow.

**Exception:** Pre-approved users.

---

### INV13: Inventory Valuation

**Rule:** Inventory valuation method consistent per item.

**Rationale:** Accounting consistency (LIFO/FIFO/Average).

**Enforcement:** Method lock on item master.

**Exception:** Change with disclosure.

---

### INV14: Fixed Asset Capitalization

**Rule:** Assets must be capitalized when ready for use.

**Rationale:** GAAP/IFRS compliance.

**Enforcement:** PIA account monitoring.

**Exception:** None.

---

### INV15: Audit Trail Immutability

**Rule:** Audit trail cannot be modified or deleted.

**Rationale:** Audit and compliance requirement.

**Enforcement:** Append-only audit table.

**Exception:** None.
