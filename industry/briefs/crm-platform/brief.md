# CRM Platform (B2B SaaS) - Universal-Fully Brief

## 1. Product Context

### Product Name

SalesHub Enterprise - B2B Customer Relationship Management Platform

### Product Type

Enterprise B2B CRM and Sales Automation Platform

### Target Market

- **Primary Markets:** North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific
- **Customer Segments:** B2B companies $10M-$10B revenue with complex sales cycles
- **Industries:** Technology, Manufacturing, Professional Services, Healthcare, Financial Services
- **Company Sizes:** 100-10,000+ employees
- **Sales Team Sizes:** 10-5,000+ sales representatives

### Problem Statement

B2B sales organizations face unique and complex challenges in modern business environments:

1. **Complex Sales Cycles:** Multi-stakeholder, multi-month sales processes requiring coordination across departments, geographic regions, and time zones. Decision units often include 5-7 stakeholders with different priorities and evaluation criteria.

2. **Data Silos:** Disconnected systems for sales, marketing, and customer service create fragmented customer views. Data entered in one system may not sync with others, leading to inconsistent information and missed opportunities.

3. **Forecast Accuracy:** Inaccurate sales forecasts lead to revenue planning issues, resource allocation problems, and stakeholder misalignment. Typical forecast accuracy ranges from 60-70% without proper tools and processes.

4. **Sales Productivity:** Too much time spent on administrative tasks (data entry, reporting, manual follow-ups) instead of selling activities. Industry benchmark shows reps spend only 35-40% of time on actual selling.

5. **Customer Visibility:** Fragmented view of customer interactions across channels (email, phone, meetings, social media, support tickets) makes it difficult to understand customer journey and identify opportunities.

6. **Compliance and Audit:** Need for complete audit trail of sales activities for regulatory compliance (SOX, GDPR, industry-specific regulations) and internal governance.

7. **Territory and Quota Management:** Complex rules for territory assignments, quota allocations, and compensation calculations that change frequently.

8. **Integration Complexity:** Connecting with ERP, marketing automation, support systems, and custom applications while maintaining data integrity and security.

### Solution Overview

SalesHub Enterprise provides a comprehensive, enterprise-grade CRM platform:

- **Contact and Account Management:** 360-degree customer view with complete interaction history, relationships, and hierarchy management
- **Opportunity Management:** Pipeline tracking, forecasting, stages, probability modeling, and win/loss analysis
- **Sales Automation:** Email, call logging, task automation, workflow rules, and intelligent reminders
- **Reporting and Analytics:** Real-time dashboards, custom reports, predictive forecasting, and AI-driven insights
- **Integration Hub:** Pre-built connectors for marketing, service, ERP, and custom APIs for extensibility
- **Mobile CRM:** Full functionality on iOS and Android with offline mode for field sales
- **Collaboration Tools:** Internal collaboration, account teams, and activity feeds
- **Document and Contract Management:** Template library, version control, e-signature integration
- **Lead and Campaign Management:** Lead scoring, campaign tracking, and attribution modeling
- **Territory and Quote Management:** Territory rules, quota tracking, product pricing, and quote generation

### Business Model

- **Licensing Model:** Per-user subscription with tiered pricing (Essential, Professional, Enterprise)
- **Implementation Services:** Professional services for configuration, data migration, and training
- **Partner Ecosystem:** AppExchange for third-party extensions and integrations
- **Support Tiers:** Standard, Premium, and Enterprise support packages

### Competitive Landscape

- **Direct Competitors:** Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Oracle CX
- **Differentiators:** Superior AI forecasting, easier customization, better price-performance ratio
- **Market Position:** Challenger targeting mid-market to enterprise seeking modern alternative

### KPI Calculation Methodology

**Pipeline Coverage:** Sum of all opportunity amounts weighted by stage probability divided by remaining quota for period.

**Win Rate:** (Number of won opportunities / Total closed opportunities) × 100, calculated monthly and by sales rep.

**Forecast Accuracy:** |Actual Revenue - Forecast Revenue| / Forecast Revenue × 100. Target is to be within 10% variance.

**Quota Attainment:** (Actual Sales / Assigned Quota) × 100, measured quarterly for compensation purposes.

**Lead Conversion Rate:** (Number of converted leads / Total leads) × 100, measured from lead creation to opportunity.

## 4. Core User Journeys (10+)

### J01: Lead to Opportunity Conversion

**Actors:** SDR, Lead, System

**Pre-conditions:**

- Lead has been captured from marketing campaign, website, or referral
- Lead meets initial qualification criteria (budget, authority, need, timeline)
- SDR has been assigned the lead through lead routing rules

**Flow:**

1. Lead enters system via web form, API, or import
2. Lead is scored based on demographic and behavioral data
3. Lead is routed to appropriate SDR based on territory, product, and capacity
4. SDR receives notification and reviews lead details
5. SDR contacts lead within SLA (target: 5 minutes for high priority)
6. SDR logs initial contact activity and updates lead status
7. SDR qualifies lead using BANT or similar framework
8. If qualified, SDR converts lead to opportunity
9. During conversion:
    - Account is created or matched to existing account
    - Contact is created or matched to existing contact
    - Opportunity is created with initial stage and amount
    - Lead owner becomes opportunity owner
10. If unqualified, lead is marked as disqualified with reason code
11. Opportunity is assigned to appropriate Account Executive
12. AE receives notification and begins engagement

**Post-conditions:**

- Qualified lead converted to opportunity
- Account and contact records created/updated
- Opportunity added to AE's pipeline
- Lead source and campaign tracked for attribution

**Success Criteria:**

- Lead response time < 5 minutes for high priority
- 80% of leads contacted within 24 hours
- 25% lead-to-opportunity conversion rate
- Complete lead source and campaign attribution

**Failure Scenarios:**

- Lead routing fails: Lead goes to queue, admin must manually assign
- Duplicate account not detected: Manual merge required subsequently
- Conversion fails: Lead remains in system, opportunity created separately

---

### J02: Opportunity Progression Through Sales Cycle

**Actors:** Account Executive, System

**Pre-conditions:**

- Opportunity has been created and assigned to AE
- Sales methodology and stages are configured
- Probability percentages assigned to each stage

**Flow:**

1. AE reviews opportunity details and account context
2. AE schedules initial discovery meeting with prospect
3. AE logs meeting activity and updates opportunity notes
4. AE identifies key stakeholders and adds as contacts
5. AE advances opportunity to Qualification stage
6. AE conducts needs analysis and documents requirements
7. AE advances opportunity to Solution stage
8. AE creates solution proposal or proof of concept
9. AE advances opportunity to Proposal stage
10. AE creates formal quote with pricing
11. AE advances opportunity to Negotiation stage
12. AE negotiates terms and addresses objections
13. AE advances opportunity to Closed stage
14. AE marks opportunity as Won or Lost with reason
15. If Won, AE creates contract and begins onboarding
16. If Lost, AE documents lost reason and competitor

**Post-conditions:**

- Opportunity has complete audit trail of all stages
- All activities and notes documented
- Win/loss reason captured for analysis
- Related contracts and orders created if won

**Success Criteria:**

- 90% of opportunities have complete activity history
- Average 2-3 activities logged per week per opportunity
- Stage progression follows defined methodology
- Lost reasons captured for 100% of lost opportunities

**Failure Scenarios:**

- Opportunity stuck in stage > 30 days: Alert to manager
- Missing required fields on stage change: Block progression
- Probability changed manually: Require manager approval

### J03: Forecast Submission and Review

**Actors:** Account Executive, Sales Manager, System

**Pre-conditions:**

- Forecast period has been defined (weekly, monthly, quarterly)
- Forecast categories are configured (Commit, Best Case, Pipeline)
- AE has opportunities in relevant forecast period

**Flow:**

1. System sends forecast submission reminder to AE
2. AE reviews all opportunities with close dates in forecast period
3. AE adjusts opportunity forecast category (Commit, Best Case, Pipeline)
4. AE can override opportunity probability if justified
5. AE submits forecast by deadline
6. System calculates total forecast from AE opportunities
7. Manager receives notification of AE forecast submission
8. Manager reviews AE forecast for accuracy
9. Manager can challenge specific opportunities
10. Manager and AE discuss challenged opportunities
11. AE or manager adjusts forecasts as needed
12. Manager submits rollup forecast to VP
13. Process repeats up management hierarchy
14. Final forecast locked at period close

**Post-conditions:**

- Forecast submitted and approved at all levels
- Forecast data available for executive reporting
- Historical forecast accuracy tracked for improvement

**Success Criteria:**

- 100% of reps submit forecast by deadline
- Forecast accuracy within 10% of actual
- Forecast variance analysis completed monthly

**Failure Scenarios:**

- Late submission: Escalate to manager, note as compliance issue
- Incomplete data: Block submission until required fields filled
- Significant variance: Require explanation and manager approval

**Post-conditions:**

- Quote engagement tracked

**Success Criteria:**

## 7. Domain Rules and Invariants (10+)

### INV01: Opportunity Cannot Close Without Amount

**Rule:** Every opportunity must have a non-zero amount before it can be marked as closed (won or lost).

**Rationale:** Revenue reporting and forecasting require accurate opportunity values. Zero-value opportunities distort pipeline metrics.

**Enforcement:** System validation on stage change to closed stage.

**Exception:** None - business process requires amount tracking.

---

### INV02: Forecast Cannot Exceed Pipeline

**Rule:** The sum of forecast amounts cannot exceed the total weighted pipeline value.

**Rationale:** Forecast represents expected revenue; it cannot exceed available opportunity value. This prevents over-optimistic forecasting.

**Enforcement:** Real-time calculation and validation during forecast submission.

**Exception:** Manager override with justification for pipeline not yet captured.

---

### INV03: Lead Must Be Assigned Before Conversion

**Rule:** A lead cannot be converted unless it has an assigned owner.

**Rationale:** Accountability for lead follow-up and conversion tracking requires clear ownership.

**Enforcement:** System validation during lead conversion.

**Exception:** None - unassigned leads must be routed before conversion.

---

### INV04: Contact Must Have Account

**Rule:** All contacts must be associated with at least one account.

**Rationale:** B2B context requires organizational context for individual contacts. Account-level reporting and management depend on this relationship.

**Enforcement:** System validation on contact creation or account removal.

**Exception:** Temporary contacts during lead conversion, must be assigned within 24 hours.

---

### INV05: Quote Cannot Be Sent Without Products

**Rule:** A quote must contain at least one product line item before it can be sent to a customer.

**Rationale:** Empty quotes have no business value and represent a process error.

**Enforcement:** System validation on quote send action.

**Exception:** None.

---

### INV06: Case Status Follows State Machine

**Rule:** Case status must follow defined state transitions: New → Assigned → In Progress → Pending → Resolved → Closed.

**Rationale:** Consistent case handling ensures proper workflow and reporting. Prevents invalid state combinations.

**Enforcement:** System validation on status change with allowed transitions defined.

**Exception:** None - state machine ensures process integrity.

---

### INV07: User Cannot Access Unauthorized Records

**Rule:** Users can only access records they own, share with them, or have role-based access to.

**Rationale:** Data security and privacy require strict access control. Prevents unauthorized data access.

**Enforcement:** Row-level security on all queries, role-based access control.

**Exception:** System administrators have elevated access with audit logging.

---

### INV08: Opportunity Stage Must Progress

**Rule:** Opportunity stages cannot be moved backward without documented reason and manager approval.

**Rationale:** Prevents gaming the pipeline and ensures accurate forecasting. Backward movement often indicates data quality issues.

**Enforcement:** Stage change validation with reason field and approval workflow.

**Exception:** Manager override with documented business justification.

---

### INV09: Revenue Recognition Cannot Exceed Contract

**Rule:** Recognized revenue cannot exceed the contracted value for a given period.

**Rationale:** Revenue recognition compliance requires accurate tracking against contract terms. Prevents overstatement of revenue.

**Enforcement:** Revenue recognition calculation validation.

**Exception:** Contract amendments with proper documentation.

---

### INV10: Discount Requires Approval Above Threshold

**Rule:** Discounts exceeding predefined thresholds require manager approval before quote can be sent.

**Rationale:** Margin protection and pricing governance require approval for significant discounts.

**Enforcement:** Approval workflow triggered based on discount percentage.

**Exception:** Pre-approved discount authority for senior sales roles.

---

### INV11: Account Cannot Be Deleted With Active Opportunities

**Rule:** Accounts with open or won opportunities cannot be deleted.

**Rationale:** Data integrity requires preserving account relationships for historical analysis.

**Enforcement:** Validation check on account deletion.

**Exception:** Account merge instead of delete.

---

### INV12: Lead Source Must Be Tracked

**Rule:** All leads must have a source field populated for attribution.

**Rationale:** Marketing ROI and channel effectiveness require source tracking.

**Enforcement:** Required field on lead creation.

**Exception:** None.

---

### INV13: Territory Assignment Must Be Unique

**Rule:** Each account can only be assigned to one territory at a time.

**Rationale:** Clear ownership prevents duplicate outreach and quota conflicts.

**Enforcement:** Territory assignment validation.

**Exception:** None - conflicts resolved through reassignment process.

---

### INV14: Contract Renewal Cannot Overlap

**Rule:** Contract renewal periods cannot overlap with existing contract periods.

**Rationale:** Billing and compliance require clear contract period boundaries.

**Enforcement:** Date validation on contract renewal.

**Exception:** Contract amendments with gap periods documented.

## 15. Open Questions

### OQ01: Deployment Model

**Question:** Should we support SaaS-only, hybrid, or on-premise deployment?

**Considerations:**

- SaaS-only: Faster innovation, lower maintenance, scalability
- Hybrid: Flexibility for sensitive data, complexity
- On-premise: Customer control, high maintenance, slower updates

**Impact:** Infrastructure requirements, pricing model, support complexity

**Recommendation:** Start with SaaS-only, evaluate hybrid for enterprise subsequently

---

### OQ02: Customization Level

**Question:** How much customization should be allowed (fields, objects, workflows)?

**Considerations:**

- Low customization: Easier maintenance, faster updates, limited flexibility
- High customization: Maximum flexibility, upgrade complexity, maintenance burden

**Impact:** Technical debt, upgrade path, support complexity, time-to-value

**Recommendation:** Allow field and workflow customization, limit object customization initially

---

### OQ03: Pricing Model

**Question:** Per-user subscription, feature-based, or usage-based pricing?

**Considerations:**

- Per-user: Predictable revenue, customer growth aligned
- Feature-based: Revenue from premium features, customer segmentation
- Usage-based: Scales with value, unpredictable revenue

**Impact:** Revenue model, customer acquisition, competitive positioning

**Recommendation:** Per-user subscription with tiered feature access

---

### OQ04: Data Migration Approach

**Question:** Build migration tools or partner with third-party migration services?

**Considerations:**

- Build tools: Control, quality, development effort
- Partner services: Speed to market, quality variance, partner management

**Impact:** Customer onboarding experience, implementation timeline, support burden

**Recommendation:** Build basic migration tools, partner for complex migrations

---

### OQ05: Mobile Strategy

**Question:** Native apps, progressive web app, or both?

**Considerations:**

- Native apps: Best performance, offline capability, development cost
- PWA: Faster development, no app store, limited offline
- Both: Best experience, highest cost

**Impact:** User experience, development timeline, maintenance

**Recommendation:** Native apps for primary CRM functions, PWA for light use cases

---

### OQ06: API-First Approach

**Question:** Build as API-first or UI-first with API as afterthought?

**Considerations:**

- API-first: Better integrations, more developer-friendly, design complexity
- UI-first: Faster time-to-market, integration limitations subsequently

**Impact:** Integration capabilities, developer ecosystem, long-term flexibility

**Recommendation:** API-first approach with comprehensive API documentation

---

### OQ07: Multi-Tenant Architecture

**Question:** Shared database with tenant isolation or dedicated databases per tenant?

**Considerations:**

- Shared database: Cost efficiency, simpler operations, data isolation complexity
- Dedicated database: Better isolation, higher cost, operational complexity

**Impact:** Cost structure, security posture, scalability

**Recommendation:** Shared database with strict tenant isolation, dedicated option for enterprise

---

### OQ08: Global Expansion

**Question:** Launch globally from day one or start with specific regions?

**Considerations:**

- Global: Market opportunity, complexity, compliance burden
- Regional: Focus, lower complexity, slower growth

**Impact:** Time to market, resource allocation, compliance requirements

**Recommendation:** Start with North America and Europe, expand based on demand

---

### OQ09: Partnership Strategy

**Question:** Build integrations in-house or partner with integration platforms?

**Considerations:**

- In-house: Control, quality, development effort
- Partners: Faster coverage, dependency, quality variance

**Impact:** Integration quality, time-to-market, maintenance burden

**Recommendation:** Build core integrations in-house, partner for long-tail

---

### OQ10: Competitive Differentiation

**Question:** What should be our key differentiator from Salesforce, Dynamics, etc.?

**Considerations:**

- Price: Attractive but sustainability concerns
- Features: Requires innovation investment
- Experience: User experience focus
- Industry: Vertical specialization

**Impact:** Marketing, product roadmap, resource allocation

**Recommendation:** Focus on superior user experience and AI-driven insights at competitive price
