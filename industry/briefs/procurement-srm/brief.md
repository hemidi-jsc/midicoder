### Problem Statement

Modern enterprise procurement faces significant challenges:

1. **Spend Visibility Gap:** Organizations typically lack visibility into 30-40% of total spend (maverick spending). Without centralized visibility, companies miss consolidation opportunities and cannot negotiate effectively. Procurement leaders cannot answer basic questions: Where is money going? Who are we buying from? Are we getting best prices?

2. **Manual Procurement Processes:** Purchase requisitions, approvals, and PO creation involve excessive manual work, emails, and spreadsheets. Average PO cycle time is 5-10 days; reducing to 1-2 days improves operational efficiency. Employees frustrated with complex processes resort to maverick buying. Procurement teams overwhelmed with administrative tasks instead of strategic activities.

3. **Supplier Risk Management:** 60% of organizations experienced supplier disruptions in the past year (financial distress, quality issues, compliance failures, geopolitical events). Lack of proactive risk monitoring leads to operational disruptions. Organizations often discover supplier problems too late - after the disruption has already occurred.

4. **Sourcing Inefficiency:** RFPs and RFQs take 6-12 weeks to complete manually. Bid evaluation is inconsistent, and best value analysis is often overlooked in favor of lowest price. Category managers spend 70% of time on administrative tasks, 30% on actual strategy and supplier development.

5. **Contract Management Fragmentation:** Contracts are scattered across departments and systems. 50%+ of contract renewals are missed, costing organizations 2-5% in unnecessary spend. Legal and procurement operate in silos. Contract terms not enforced in purchasing operations.

6. **Supplier Relationship Silos:** Strategic supplier relationships are managed informally without structured performance tracking, development plans, or collaboration mechanisms. Knowledge resides in individual relationships rather than organizational systems. Supplier performance data incomplete or outdated.

7. **Compliance and Control:** Procurement compliance with internal policies and external regulations is difficult to enforce. Audit preparation is manual and time-consuming. SOX, GDPR, FCPA compliance requirements create additional burden.

8. **Integration Complexity:** Connecting procurement with ERP, finance, inventory, and quality systems creates data silos and reconciliation challenges. Data inconsistencies between systems cause operational delays and errors.

9. **Tail Spend Management:** Organizations typically have thousands of low-value suppliers representing 80% of supplier count but only 20% of spend. These relationships are unmanaged, unconsolidated, and represent significant risk and opportunity.

10. **Sustainability Requirements:** Growing regulatory and stakeholder pressure for sustainable sourcing. Organizations lack visibility into supplier environmental and social practices. ESG scoring and reporting capabilities absent.

### Solution Overview

ProcureLink Pro provides a comprehensive cloud-based procurement and SRM platform:

- **Spend Analysis:** Consolidate and analyze spend data from multiple sources to identify savings opportunities. ML-powered categorization. Anomaly detection for maverick spend. Benchmarking against industry peers.

- **Strategic Sourcing:** End-to-end RFP/RFQ management with collaboration tools and award recommendations. Reverse auctions for commoditized categories. Supplier pre-qualification workflows.

- **Procurement Operations:** Purchase requisition to payment (P2P) with workflow automation and approval routing. Delegated approval support. Budget integration. Touchless processing for compliant transactions.

- **Supplier Management:** Centralized supplier database with qualification, risk monitoring, and performance tracking. External data feeds for financial health, news, compliance events. Self-service supplier portal.

- **Contract Management:** Contract lifecycle management from negotiation to renewal with compliance tracking. Redline comparison. Obligation management. Automated renewal alerts.

- **Supplier Portal:** Self-service portal for suppliers to manage profiles, bids, POs, and performance. Mobile access. Multi-language support. Integration with supplier systems.

- **Analytics and Reporting:** Real-time dashboards, spend analysis, savings tracking, and supplier scorecards. Custom report builder. Executive summaries. Regulatory reports.

- **Integration Platform:** Pre-built connectors for major ERP systems (SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Microsoft). API-first architecture. Event-driven integration support.

- **AI and Automation:** Intelligent catalog recommendations. Automated bid evaluation. Anomaly detection. Predictive risk analytics. Chatbot for requisitioners.

## 4. Core User Journeys

### J01: Purchase Requisition to PO

**Primary Persona:** P05 (Amanda Garcia - Requisitioner) → P03 (Jennifer Williams - Procurement Specialist)

**Trigger Event:** Employee needs to purchase goods or services for their department.

**Preconditions:**

- Employee has requisitioning access granted
- Catalog items or suppliers available in system
- Budget is available in the specified cost center
- Approval workflow is configured for the requisition type

**Postconditions:**

- PO is created and sent to supplier
- Budget is committed (reserved)
- Requester notified of PO creation and tracking
- Supplier acknowledges PO (if EDI integrated)

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. **Request Initiation:**
    - Employee logs into procurement system via SSO
    - Navigates to "Create Requisition" from dashboard
    - Searches catalog for items by name, category, or supplier
    - Adds items to cart with quantities, delivery dates
    - System shows real-time pricing and availability

2. **Catalog Selection:**
    - System shows approved catalog items with contract pricing
    - Lead times and supplier information displayed
    - Alternative items suggested if preferred unavailable
    - Non-catalog option available with additional justification

3. **Requisition Creation:**
    - Employee reviews cart and total amount
    - Adds delivery information (location, contact)
    - Assigns cost center and GL code
    - Adds business justification if required
    - Submits requisition for approval

4. **Approval Routing:**
    - System determines approval workflow based on:
        - Amount thresholds
        - Item categories
        - Cost center
        - User's approval hierarchy
    - First approver notified via email and in-app
    - Approver can approve, reject, or request changes
    - Route to next approver if serial approval needed
    - Parallel approval if multiple approvers at same level

5. **Procurement Processing:**
    - Approved requisition routes to procurement queue
    - System suggests PO creation (touchless for low-risk)
    - Procurement specialist reviews non-touchless items
    - System recommends suppliers based on:
        - Category
        - Contract status
        - Performance rating
        - Delivery capability

6. **PO Creation:**
    - PO created from approved requisition
    - Supplier selected and confirmed
    - Delivery terms and payment terms confirmed
    - PO number assigned and formatted per supplier requirements
    - PO sent to supplier via preferred method (EDI, portal, email)

7. **Notification:**
    - Requester notified of PO creation with tracking link
    - PO number and expected delivery shown
    - Delivery tracking available if supplier provides
    - Finance notified for budget commitment

**Alternative Paths:**

- **Touchless PO:** Auto-create PO without procurement review for:
    - Catalog items with contracts
    - Amounts below threshold
    - Low-risk suppliers
    - Compliant requisitions

- **Non-Catalog:** For non-catalog items:
    - Supplier selection required
    - Additional approval layers
    - Justification documentation

- **Emergency:** Expedited approval workflow:
    - Mobile approvals enabled
    - Reduced approval steps
    - Post-approval documentation required

- **BLANKET PO:** Create release against existing blanket agreement:
    - Select blanket contract
    - Specify release quantity
    - Confirm terms match blanket

### CR04: OFAC Trade Sanctions Screening

**Regulation:** Office of Foreign Assets Control (31 CFR Chapter V)

**Purpose:** Screen suppliers and transactions against US government sanctions lists to prevent trade with sanctioned entities, individuals, and countries.

**Requirements:**

- **Sanctions Lists Coverage:**
    - Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) List
    - Consolidated Sanctions List
    - Foreign Sanctions Evaders (FSE) List
    - Sectoral Sanctions Identifications (SSI) List
    - Non-SDN Palestinian Legislative Council List
    - Non-SDN Menu-Based Sanctions List
    - Blocked Entities List (Entity List)
    - Unverified List (UVL)

- **Screening Points:**
    - Supplier onboarding (before qualification)
    - Supplier master data updates
    - Purchase order creation
    - Payment processing
    - Contract execution
    - Periodic re-screening (quarterly minimum)

- **Entity Types to Screen:**
    - Supplier legal entities
    - Supplier beneficial owners (>25% ownership)
    - Supplier key personnel (executives, directors)
    - End-user/customer (for resale items)
    - Shipping destinations
    - Bank accounts (for payment processing)

- **Match Criteria:**
    - Name matching (exact and fuzzy)
    - Tax ID/EIN matching
    - Address matching
    - Date of birth (for individuals)
    - Passport number (for individuals)
    - Business registration numbers

- **Risk-Based Approach:**
    - High-risk: Sanctioned countries (Iran, North Korea, Syria, Cuba, Crimea)
    - Medium-risk: Dual-use goods, military applications
    - Low-risk: Standard commercial transactions

- **Actions on Match:**
    - **Exact Match:** Block transaction, escalate to compliance officer, file report if required
    - **Potential Match:** Manual review required before proceeding
    - **False Positive:** Document rationale, allow with approval

- **Reporting Requirements:**
    - Blocked transaction reporting to OFAC (10 days)
    - Suspicious activity documentation
    - Audit trail of all screening activities
    - Annual compliance certification

**Implementation:**

- Real-time screening API integration (refinitiv, worldcheck, or government APIs)
- Automated screening at supplier onboarding
- PO-level screening before approval
- Payment screening before release
- Scheduled batch re-screening of supplier database
- Match resolution workflow with escalation paths
- OFAC match documentation and reporting
- Screening exception management with approval workflow

**Evidence:**

- Screening policy documentation
- Screening logs with timestamps
- Match resolution records
- OFAC reports filed (if applicable)
- Compliance audit results
- Training records

**Penalties for Non-Compliance:**

- Civil penalties: $272,391 per violation or 2x transaction value
- Criminal penalties: Up to $1,000,000+ and 20 years imprisonment
- Debarment from government contracts
- Reputational damage
