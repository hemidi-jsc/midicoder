# Omnichannel Retail Platform - Universal-Fully Brief

## 1. Product Context

### Product Name

OmniRetail Pro - Unified Commerce Platform

### Product Type

Omnichannel Retail Management Platform with Unified Commerce Capabilities

### Target Market

- **Primary Markets:** North America, Western Europe, Australia, Southeast Asia
- **Customer Segments:** Retailers with 10-500 stores, $5M-$500M annual revenue
- **Retail Categories:** Apparel & Fashion, Home Goods, Electronics, Sporting Goods, Beauty

### Problem Statement

Omnichannel retail requires seamless integration across physical stores, e-commerce, mobile, social commerce, and marketplaces:

1. **Channel Fragmentation:** Retailers operate multiple channels (store, web, mobile, marketplaces) with disconnected systems creating inconsistent customer experiences.

2. **Inventory Visibility:** Customers expect real-time inventory visibility across all channels. Stockouts, overselling, and inability to locate products drive frustration.

3. **Order Orchestration:** Fulfilling orders from optimal locations (store, warehouse, vendor) requires sophisticated orchestration to balance speed, cost, and accuracy.

4. **Customer Identity:** Recognizing customers across channels requires unified identity management. Without it, personalization and loyalty programs fail.

5. **Inconsistent Pricing/Promotions:** Maintaining consistent pricing, promotions, and product information across channels is operationally complex.

6. **Store Operations:** Store associates need tools to access customer data, inventory, and order capabilities to deliver white-glove service.

### Solution Overview

OmniRetail Pro provides:

- **Unified Product Information Management (PIM):** Single source of truth for product data across all channels
- **Real-Time Inventory Management:** Global inventory visibility with location-level granularity
- **Intelligent Order Orchestration:** Rule-based order routing to optimal fulfillment locations
- **Customer Data Platform (CDP):** Unified customer profiles across all touchpoints
- **Flexible Fulfillment Options:** Ship-to-home, BOPIS (Buy Online, Pick Up In-Store), BORIS (Buy Online, Return In-Store), Ship-from-Store
- **Store Associate Tools:** Mobile POS, customer lookup, inventory search, order management
- **Omnichannel Loyalty:** Unified loyalty program with seamless redemption across channels
- **Unified Promotions Engine:** Consistent promotions with channel-specific rules

### Business Model

- **Revenue Model:**
    - **Platform Subscription:** $5,000-$50,000/month based on store count and transaction volume
    - **Implementation Services:** $50,000-$500,000 one-time
    - **Transaction Fees:** $0.10-$0.50 per transaction for payment processing
    - **Add-On Modules:** $1,000-$10,000/month (AI recommendations, advanced analytics)
    - **Annual Maintenance:** 20% of subscription

- **Deployment Models:**
    - **SaaS:** Multi-tenant cloud deployment
    - **Private Cloud:** Dedicated instance
    - **Hybrid:** Cloud core with on-premise store systems

### Go-to-Market Strategy

- **Phase 1 (Months 1-6):** 20 customers, focus on apparel retailers with 10-50 stores
- **Phase 2 (Months 7-18):** 100 customers, expand to home goods and electronics
- **Phase 3 (Months 19-36):** 500+ customers, marketplace expansion

## 4. Core User Journeys

### J01: Customer Researches Online, Buys In-Store

**Primary Persona:** P07 (Customer - Anna Brown)

**Trigger Event:** Customer sees product online and wants to purchase in-store

**Preconditions:**

- Product is in catalog
- Customer has account or can identify in-store
- Store has inventory

**Postconditions:**

- Customer purchases product in-store
- Online browsing history linked to customer profile
- Loyalty points applied if member

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. Customer browses products on e-commerce site
2. Customer adds items to wishlist
3. Customer checks store availability via website
4. Customer visits store location
5. Customer identifies self (loyalty card, phone number, or app)
6. Store associate accesses customer profile on mobile device
7. Associate sees customer's wishlist from online session
8. Associate confirms product availability on sales floor
9. Customer and associate go to product location
10. Customer tries product and decides to purchase
11. Associate processes sale via mobile POS
12. Loyalty points are applied automatically
13. Customer receives email receipt
14. Online wishlist is updated (item removed)

**Alternative Paths:**

- **Anonymous shopper:** Customer not identified, no wishlist access
- **Out of stock in-store:** Associate arranges ship-from-store or transfer
- **Price match:** Associate matches online price if lower

**Success Criteria:**

- Wishlist recovery rate > 50%
- Cross-channel recognition rate > 80%
- Transaction time < 5 minutes

**Metrics Tracked:**

- Online-to-store conversion rate
- Cross-channel purchase rate
- Wishlist-to-purchase rate

**Failure Scenarios:**

| Scenario                   | Expected Handling                                      |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Customer not identified    | Associate proceeds with anonymous transaction          |
| Wishlist item out of stock | Associate offers alternatives or endless aisle         |
| Price mismatch             | Associate verifies online price and matches per policy |

### J02: Buy Online, Pick Up In-Store (BOPIS)

**Primary Persona:** P07 (Customer) with P02 (Store Manager) and P03 (Store Associate)

**Trigger Event:** Customer orders online and selects in-store pickup

**Preconditions:**

- Customer has account
- Store has inventory
- BOPIS is enabled for store and product

**Postconditions:**

- Order is fulfilled and ready for pickup
- Customer receives product
- Inventory is updated

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. Customer browses products online
2. Customer adds items to cart
3. Customer selects "Pick Up In-Store" at checkout
4. Customer selects preferred store location
5. System confirms real-time inventory at selected store
6. Customer completes payment
7. Order is transmitted to store system
8. Store receives notification
9. Store associate receives pick list
10. Associate locates and picks items from sales floor or backstock
11. Associate bags items and moves to pickup area
12. System marks order as "Ready for Pickup"
13. Customer receives notification with pickup instructions
14. Customer arrives at store within pickup window
15. Customer goes to pickup counter or curbside
16. Associate verifies customer identity
17. Associate hands over order
18. Customer receives confirmation and receipt

**Alternative Paths:**

- **Out of stock at pickup:** Store transfers from nearby store or cancels
- **Customer late:** Order held for X days, then returned to stock
- **Customer requests change:** Substitute product or ship-to-home

**Success Criteria:**

- Pick time < 30 minutes for store
- Ready for pickup time < 2 hours
- BOPIS conversion rate > 95%
- Pickup time at store < 5 minutes

**Metrics Tracked:**

- BOPIS order volume
- BOPIS conversion rate
- Pick time SLA
- Pickup time at counter

**Failure Scenarios:**

| Scenario                      | Expected Handling                                             |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Item out of stock after order | Find in nearby store, ship to customer, or cancel with refund |
| Customer doesn't pick up      | Hold per policy, then return to stock                         |
| Customer can't identify       | Verify via order confirmation email or SMS code               |

### J03: Ship-from-Store Fulfillment

**Primary Persona:** P02 (Store Manager) and P03 (Store Associate)

**Trigger Event:** Online order routed to store for fulfillment

**Preconditions:**

- Order orchestration rules route to store
- Store has inventory
- Store has shipping capability

**Postconditions:**

- Order is shipped
- Customer receives product
- Store inventory is updated

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. Customer places order online
2. Order orchestration engine evaluates fulfillment options
3. Store is selected as optimal fulfillment location
4. Order is transmitted to store system
5. Store receives order notification
6. Associate receives pick list
7. Associate picks items from sales floor or backstock
8. Associate stages items at shipping station
9. Associate scans items to confirm pick
10. System reserves inventory (prevents oversell)
11. Associate packages order according to guidelines
12. Associate prints shipping label
13. Associate attaches label and places in carrier bin
14. System marks order as "Shipped"
15. Customer receives shipment notification
16. Carrier picks up package
17. Inventory is decremented

**Alternative Paths:**

- **Out of stock:** System reroutes to alternate location
- **Shipping delay:** Order held and customer notified
- **Package damaged:** Associate re-packages

**Success Criteria:**

- Ship time < 4 hours from order
- Pick accuracy > 99%
- Ship-from-store rate > 30%

**Metrics Tracked:**

- Ship-from-store volume
- Ship time SLA
- Pick accuracy rate
- Shipping cost per order

**Failure Scenarios:**

| Scenario                    | Expected Handling                                 |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Shipping label printer down | Use alternate printer or mobile label printing    |
| Carrier pickup missed       | Reschedule pickup, drop off at location           |
| Wrong item picked           | Scan verification prevents, or recall before ship |

## 7. Domain Rules and Invariants

### INV01: Inventory Consistency

- **Name:** Available Inventory Cannot Be Negative
- **Description:** The available-to-promise inventory must never be negative unless backorder is enabled
- **Formal Statement:** `∀p ∈ Products, ∀l ∈ Locations, (p.backorder_enabled AT l OR p.available_qty AT l >= 0)`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (before reservation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `INSUFFICIENT_INVENTORY`; block reservation

### INV02: Order Total Integrity

- **Name:** Order Total Must Be Consistent
- **Description:** Order total must equal sum of line items plus tax, shipping, minus discounts
- **Formal Statement:** `order.total = sum(order.lines.price * qty) + tax + shipping - discounts`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (before order confirmation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `ORDER_TOTAL_MISMATCH`; reject order

### INV03: Payment Before Fulfillment

- **Name:** Payment Must Be Secured Before Fulfillment
- **Description:** Orders cannot be fulfilled without payment authorization
- **Formal Statement:** `∀o ∈ Orders, o.fulfilled → o.payment_status IN (authorized, captured)`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on fulfillment)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `PAYMENT_NOT_SECURED`; block fulfillment

### INV04: No Over-Refund

- **Name:** Refunds Cannot Exceed Order Total
- **Description:** Total refunds cannot exceed original order total
- **Formal Statement:** `∀o ∈ Orders, sum(o.refunds.amount) <= o.total`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (before refund)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `OVER_REFUND`; reject refund

### INV05: Loyalty Points Conservation

- **Name:** Points Cannot Be Created or Destroyed Arbitrarily
- **Description:** Loyalty points must be earned and redeemed with audit trail
- **Formal Statement:** `∀c ∈ Customers, c.points = sum(c.earnings) - sum(c.redemptions)`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on point transaction)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `INVALID_POINT_TRANSACTION`; log and reject

### INV06: Customer Data Isolation

- **Name:** Customer Data Is Isolated by Organization
- **Description:** Customer data cannot be accessed across organizations
- **Formal Statement:** `∀c ∈ Customers, ∀u ∈ Users, user.org != customer.org → access(u, c) = denied`
- **Enforcement Point:** Compile-time + Runtime
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `UNAUTHORIZED_ACCESS`; security incident

### INV07: Price Floor Enforcement

- **Name:** Prices Cannot Fall Below Floor
- **Description:** Prices cannot be set below configured floor without approval
- **Formal Statement:** `∀p ∈ Products, p.price >= p.floor_price OR exception_approved`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on price update)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `BELOW_PRICE_FLOOR`; require approval

### INV08: Promotion Validity

- **Name:** Promotions Cannot Apply Outside Valid Period
- **Description:** Promotions only apply during configured date/time range
- **Formal Statement:** `∀promo ∈ Promotions, promo.active → (now >= promo.start AND now <= promo.end)`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on promotion evaluation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `PROMOTION_NOT_ACTIVE`; do not apply

### INV09: Return Window Enforcement

- **Name:** Returns Must Be Within Allowed Window
- **Description:** Returns cannot be processed after return window expires
- **Formal Statement:** `∀r ∈ Returns, r.submitted_at <= order.shipped_at + return_window`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on return submission)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `RETURN_WINDOW_EXPIRED`; deny return

### INV10: Stock Reservation Consistency

- **Name:** Reserved Inventory Must Be Released or Converted
- **Description:** Inventory reservations must either convert to order or be released
- **Formal Statement:** `∀res ∈ Reservations, res.expired OR res.converted OR res.released`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (periodic cleanup)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `STALE_RESERVATION`; auto-release

### INV11: Order Status State Machine

- **Name:** Order Status Follows Valid Transitions
- **Description:** Order status can only transition through defined states
- **Formal Statement:** `∀o ∈ Orders, o.status ∈ {created, paid, processing, shipped, delivered, cancelled, refunded}`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on status change)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `INVALID_STATUS_TRANSITION`; reject change

### INV12: Store Commission Accuracy

- **Name:** Store Commission Must Be Accurate
- **Description:** Store commission for omnichannel orders must be calculated correctly
- **Formal Statement:** `commission = order.total * commission_rate`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on order completion)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `COMMISSION_CALCULATION_ERROR`; recalculate

### INV13: Gift Card Balance Integrity

- **Name:** Gift Card Balance Cannot Be Negative
- **Description:** Gift card balance must never go negative
- **Formal Statement:** `∀g ∈ GiftCards, g.balance >= 0`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on redemption)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `INSUFFICIENT_GIFT_CARD_BALANCE`; decline partial

### INV14: Appointment Slot Uniqueness

- **Name:** Appointment Slots Cannot Be Double-Booked
- **Description:** Each appointment slot can only have one customer assigned
- **Formal Statement:** `∀a1, a2 ∈ Appointments, a1.slot = a2.slot → a1.customer = a2.customer`
- **Enforcement Point:** Runtime (on appointment creation)
- **Violation Handling:** Error code `SLOT_ALREADY_BOOKED`; offer alternate
