# Marketplace B2B Platform - Universal-Fully Brief

## 1. Product Context

### Product Name

TradeHub Enterprise - B2B Procurement Marketplace

### Product Type

B2B Multi-Vendor Marketplace for Wholesale Procurement and Business Supply Chain

### Target Market

- **Primary Markets:** North America (US, Canada), Western Europe (UK, Germany, France), Asia (Singapore, Japan, South Korea)
- **Customer Segments:** Businesses requiring bulk procurement ($1M-$100M annual procurement spend)
- **Product Categories:** Industrial Equipment, Office Supplies, Raw Materials, Packaging, MRO Supplies, Electronics Components, Chemicals, Building Materials
- **Target Buyer Organizations:** Manufacturing companies, wholesalers, distributors, retail chains, construction firms, healthcare facilities, educational institutions

### Problem Statement

B2B procurement marketplaces face unique challenges distinct from B2C:

1. **Complex Pricing Structures:** B2B pricing involves volume tiers (5+ levels), negotiated contracts, customer-specific pricing, contract-based pricing, and dynamic pricing that changes based on relationship history, volume commitments, and seasonal factors. A single product may have 50+ different prices for different customers.

2. **Extended Sales Cycles:** B2B purchases involve multiple stakeholders (procurement, finance, operations, technical, legal) with approval workflows spanning weeks or months. A $500K purchase order may require 5+ approvers and 30+ days to complete the approval chain.

3. **Credit and Payment Terms:** B2B transactions typically use net-30/60/90 terms, credit lines up to $10M, purchase orders, and consignment arrangements rather than immediate payment. Managing receivables, collections, and credit risk is critical.

4. **Complex Product Specifications:** B2B products require detailed technical specifications (50+ attributes), compliance documentation (certificates, SDS sheets), certification verification (ISO, UL, CE), and compatibility matrices that B2C buyers don't need.

5. **Integration Requirements:** B2B buyers need integration with ERP (SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics), procurement systems (Coupa, Ariba, Procurify), and accounting software (QuickBooks Enterprise, NetSuite). Manual re-entry is not acceptable.

6. **Contract Management:** Long-term framework agreements (1-3 years), price locks, volume commitments, and take-or-pay contracts require sophisticated contract management with automatic renewal, compliance tracking, and dispute resolution mechanisms.

7. **Multi-Location Deliveries:** Orders often ship to multiple warehouse locations, job sites, or retail stores with complex delivery scheduling, receiving procedures, and proof of delivery requirements.

8. **Regulatory Compliance:** Industry-specific regulations (OSHA, EPA, FDA, REACH) affect product eligibility, documentation requirements, and restricted party screening that must be enforced at order time.

### Solution Overview

TradeHub Enterprise provides:

- **Complex Pricing Engine:** Volume tiers (unlimited levels), customer-specific pricing matrices, contract pricing with auto-application, quote-based pricing, and currency-specific pricing tables
- **B2B Checkout:** Purchase order workflows, credit terms management, multi-level approval chains, PO numbering conventions, and integration with buyer's financial systems
- **Enterprise Integration:** Pre-built connectors for SAP, Oracle ERP, Microsoft Dynamics, NetSuite, Coupa, Ariba, and custom API for other systems
- **Quote Management:** Request for Quote (RFQ) with specifications, multi-supplier bidding, quote comparison matrices, negotiation tracking, and conversion to orders
- **Contract Management:** Framework agreements with terms tracking, price lock enforcement, volume commitment monitoring, automatic renewal alerts, and compliance dashboards
- **Multi-User Workflows:** Role-based access control, approval chain configuration, delegation rules, mobile approvals, and audit trails
- **Advanced Analytics:** Spend analysis by category/vendor, supplier performance scorecards, cost savings tracking, compliance dashboards, and predictive analytics
- **Multi-Location Fulfillment:** Split shipments, delivery scheduling, receiving confirmation, proof of delivery capture, and partial shipment handling

### Business Model

- **Revenue Model:**
    - **Transaction Commission:** 2-8% per transaction (lower than B2C due to higher values, tiered by category)
    - **Seller Subscription:** $499-$4,999/month for premium seller features (enhanced analytics, API access, dedicated support)
    - **Buyer Subscription:** $299-$2,999/month for premium buyer analytics and advanced features
    - **Financing Fees:** 1.5-3% interest on purchase order financing and invoice factoring services
    - **Value-Added Services:** Logistics coordination (1-3%), quality inspection ($50-$500 per inspection), insurance brokerage (0.5-1%)
    - **Listing Fees:** Featured listings and category prominence ($500-$5,000/month)
    - **Data Services:** Benchmark reports and market intelligence ($10,000-$100,000/year)

- **Pricing Tiers for Suppliers:**
    - **Starter:** Free listing, 8% commission, basic analytics, email support
    - **Professional:** $499/month, 6% commission, enhanced analytics, phone support
    - **Enterprise:** $4,999/month, 4% commission, API access, dedicated account manager, custom integrations

- **Pricing Tiers for Buyers:**
    - **Starter:** Free for organizations under $1M annual spend
    - **Growth:** $299/month for $1M-$10M annual spend
    - **Enterprise:** $2,999/month for $10M+ annual spend (includes dedicated support and custom features)

## 4. Core User Journeys

### J01: Buyer Organization Onboarding

**Primary Persona:** P01 (Robert Chen - Procurement Manager)

**Trigger Event:** Company decides to use marketplace for procurement

**Preconditions:**

- Company has valid business registration and tax ID
- Company has authorized representatives with signing authority
- Company has payment method or credit arrangement ready
- Company has identified initial procurement needs

**Postconditions:**

- Buyer organization is verified and active on platform
- Users are created with appropriate roles and permissions
- Credit terms are established and approved
- Payment methods are configured
- ERP integration is set up (if applicable)
- Organization can place first order

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. Procurement manager visits marketplace and clicks "Register as Buyer"
2. Manager enters organization information:
    - Legal business name and DBA
    - Tax ID (EIN) and business license number
    - Business address and phone
    - Website and industry classification
3. Manager creates admin account with email verification
4. Manager uploads verification documents:
    - Business registration certificate
    - Tax ID verification
    - Proof of address (utility bill or bank statement)
    - Authorized signatory documentation
5. Manager completes credit application:
    - Requested credit limit
    - Preferred payment terms (Net-30, Net-60, etc.)
    - Banking information for ACH
    - Financial references (optional)
6. Platform performs business verification (automated):
    - Tax ID validation
    - Business registration check
    - Sanctions and restricted party screening
7. Platform performs credit check:
    - Business credit report (Dun & Bradstreet, Experian)
    - Bank reference verification
    - Credit limit recommendation
8. Credit approval (automated or manual review):
    - Approval decision within 24-72 hours
    - Credit limit assigned
    - Payment terms confirmed
9. Manager configures organization settings:
    - Currency preference
    - Tax-exempt status (if applicable)
    - PO numbering convention
    - Default shipping addresses
10. Manager creates user accounts for team members:
    - Add users with email invitations
    - Assign roles and permissions
    - Configure approval workflows
11. Manager sets up ERP integration (if applicable):
    - Select integration type (SAP, NetSuite, etc.)
    - Provide API credentials
    - Test connection
    - Configure data mappings
12. Manager completes onboarding tutorial
13. Organization is activated and ready to order

**Alternative Paths:**

- **Fast-track onboarding:** Pre-vetted buyers (enterprise accounts) skip credit check
- **Agency-assisted:** Implementation partner completes onboarding on behalf of buyer
- **Trial account:** Limited functionality account for evaluation period
- **Bulk user import:** Upload users via CSV for large organizations

**Success Criteria:**

- Standard onboarding completion time < 48 hours
- Credit approval time < 72 hours
- Onboarding completion rate > 85%
- First order placed within 7 days of activation

**Metrics Tracked:**

- Onboarding funnel conversion rate (by step)
- Time to credit approval
- Time to first order
- Onboarding abandonment points
- Integration setup rate

**Failure Scenarios and Handling:**

| Failure Scenario            | Expected Handling                                                                  |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Business verification fails | Clear error message with required documents, option to appeal                      |
| Credit application rejected | Explanation of reasons, option to request review or provide additional information |
| Document upload fails       | Retry mechanism, alternative upload methods, support contact                       |

- Supplier has valid business registration and tax ID
- Supplier has products/services to offer

**Postconditions:**

- Pricing is configured
- Payment/payout method is configured
- Supplier can receive and fulfill orders

**Step-by-Step Flow:**

1. Supplier visits marketplace and clicks "Register as Supplier"
2. Supplier enters business information:
    - Company legal name and trade name
    - Tax ID and business license
    - Business address and contact information
    - Industry and product categories
3. Supplier creates account with email verification
4. Supplier uploads verification documents:
    - Business registration and tax documents
    - Product certifications (ISO, UL, CE, etc.)
    - Insurance certificate (general liability, product liability)
    - Banking information for payouts
5. Supplier completes capability questionnaire:
    - Product categories offered
    - Minimum order quantities
    - Shipping capabilities and regions
    - Lead times and fulfillment capacity
    - Quality certifications
6. Supplier submits product catalog:
    - Product listings with SKUs
    - Product specifications and attributes
    - Product images and documentation
    - Pricing structure (base prices, volume tiers)
7. Platform performs supplier verification:
    - Business verification
    - Certificate validation
    - Insurance verification
    - Background check
8. Platform reviews product catalog:
    - Quality and completeness check
    - Category assignment
    - Compliance verification
9. Supplier completes payout setup:
    - Bank account verification
    - Payout frequency selection
    - Tax form (W-9 or W-8)
10. Supplier agrees to terms and policies:
    - Supplier agreement
    - Service level commitments
    - Dispute resolution process
    - Code of conduct
11. Background and reference checks (for high-value categories):
    - Customer references verification
    - Financial stability check
12. Supplier receives approval decision:
    - Approval with any conditions
    - Rejection with reasons and appeal process
13. Upon approval, supplier completes onboarding training:
    - Platform navigation
    - Order management

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements

### Security

**NFR01: Data Encryption**

- **Requirement:** All sensitive data must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3
- **Details:**
    - Database encryption at rest for all tables containing PII, financial data, credentials
    - Encryption keys managed via AWS KMS or equivalent
    - TLS 1.3 for all external communication
    - TLS for internal service-to-service communication
    - Certificate rotation every 90 days
- **Measurement:** Security audit verification, encryption coverage = 100%

**NFR02: Authentication Security**

- **Requirement:** Strong authentication with MFA support and SSO for enterprise
- **Details:**
    - Password complexity enforcement (min 12 chars, complexity requirements)
    - TOTP-based 2FA support
    - SAML 2.0 SSO support
    - OAuth 2.0 for API authentication
    - Session timeout after 30 minutes inactivity
    - Account lockout after 5 failed attempts
- **Measurement:** Penetration test results, auth success/failure metrics

**NFR03: API Security**

- **Requirement:** All API endpoints must be authenticated, authorized, and rate-limited
- **Details:**
    - OAuth 2.0 bearer tokens for API access
    - Rate limiting: 1000 requests/minute per API key
    - Input validation and sanitization
    - SQL injection prevention
    - XSS prevention
    - CORS configuration
- **Measurement:** API security scan results, rate limit enforcement verification

**NFR04: Data Isolation**

- **Requirement:** Complete data isolation between tenant organizations
- **Details:**
    - Tenant ID on all records
    - Row-level security enforcement
    - API filtering by tenant

## 7. Domain Rules and Invariants

### INV01: Price Non-Negativity

- **Rule:** All prices in the system must be non-negative values
- **Rationale:** Negative prices indicate data errors or system bugs
- **Enforcement:** Validation at API and database level
- **Exception:** None

### INV02: Order Total Integrity

- **Rule:** Order total must equal sum of line items plus taxes and shipping minus discounts
- **Rationale:** Financial accuracy and audit compliance
- **Enforcement:** Calculation on order creation, validation on payment
- **Exception:** Rounding differences up to $0.01 acceptable

### INV03: Credit Limit Enforcement

- **Rule:** Orders cannot exceed customer credit limit without explicit approval
- **Rationale:** Financial risk management
- **Enforcement:** Validation at checkout, exception workflow for overrides
- **Exception:** Credit increase approval, emergency override with documentation

### INV04: Approval Before Order Submission

- **Rule:** Orders exceeding configured threshold must be approved before submission to supplier
- **Rationale:** Financial controls and policy compliance
- **Enforcement:** Workflow enforcement, cannot bypass without proper authorization
- **Exception:** Emergency approval with post-approval documentation

### INV05: Invoice-Order Match

- **Rule:** Invoice amounts must match order amounts within configured tolerance (default 1%)
- **Rationale:** Payment accuracy and fraud prevention
- **Enforcement:** 3-way matching, exception handling for variances
- **Exception:** Approved variance, price adjustment with documentation

### INV06: Contract Pricing Enforcement

- **Rule:** Orders placed under active contract must use contract pricing
- **Rationale:** Contract compliance and legal obligations
- **Enforcement:** Price lookup with contract priority, validation on order
- **Exception:** Contract amendment, special approval for deviation

### INV07: No Over-Refund

- **Rule:** Total refunds and credits cannot exceed original order value
- **Rationale:** Financial integrity and fraud prevention
- **Enforcement:** Running total tracking, validation on refund creation
- **Exception:** Exceptional circumstances with executive approval

### INV08: Quote Expiry Enforcement

- **Rule:** Quotes must have expiry dates and cannot be accepted after expiry
- **Rationale:** Price accuracy and supplier protection
- **Enforcement:** Expiry date required, validation on quote acceptance
- **Exception:** Quote extension by supplier

### INV09: Supplier Data Isolation

- **Rule:** Suppliers cannot see other suppliers' pricing, customer data, or order details
- **Rationale:** Competitive protection and privacy
- **Enforcement:** Data filtering at API and UI level, tenant isolation
- **Exception:** Platform admin with proper authorization

### INV10: Audit Log Immutability

- **Rule:** Audit log entries cannot be modified or deleted after creation
- **Rationale:** Compliance and forensic investigation
- **Enforcement:** WORM storage, no delete/update operations on audit table
- **Exception:** None

### INV11: PO Number Uniqueness

- **Rule:** Each purchase order number must be unique within organization
- **Rationale:** Order tracking and accounting reconciliation
- **Enforcement:** Unique constraint, auto-generation with sequence
- **Exception:** None

### INV12: Order Status State Machine

- **Rule:** Order status must follow defined state transitions
- **Rationale:** Process integrity and workflow correctness
- **Enforcement:** State machine validation, invalid transitions rejected
- **Exception:** Admin override with audit trail

### INV13: Inventory Consistency

- **Rule:** Inventory adjustments must maintain non-negative available quantity (unless backorder allowed)
- **Rationale:** Order fulfillment accuracy
- **Enforcement:** Validation on inventory change, reservation system
- **Exception:** Backorder mode enabled, negative inventory adjustment with reason

### INV14: User Organization Binding

- **Rule:** Users must be bound to exactly one organization (buyer or supplier)
- **Rationale:** Access control and data isolation
- **Enforcement:** Organization ID required on user record, cannot be null
- **Exception:** Platform users (not bound to buyer/supplier org)

### INV15: Currency Consistency

- **Rule:** All amounts in an order must be in the same currency
- **Rationale:** Financial accuracy and accounting
- **Enforcement:** Currency validation on order creation, conversion before finalization
- **Exception:** Multi-currency orders with explicit conversion and approval

## 15. Open Questions

### OQ01: Financing Model

- **Question:** Should platform offer embedded financing directly or partner with third-party financiers?
- **Impact:** High - affects revenue model, compliance requirements, technical complexity
- **Options:**
    - Direct financing (requires lending licenses, capital)
    - Partner marketplace (revenue share, lower risk)
    - Referral only (lowest complexity, lowest revenue)
- **Decision Owner:** CFO / VP Product
- **Decision Deadline:** Before Phase 3 planning

### OQ02: Dispute Liability

- **Question:** Who bears liability for quality disputes - platform, buyer, or supplier?
- **Impact:** High - affects risk exposure, terms of service, insurance requirements
- **Options:**
    - Platform as neutral arbiter (no liability)
    - Platform guarantee (limited liability insurance)
    - Pass-through to supplier (buyer/supplier direct)
- **Decision Owner:** Legal / VP Trust & Safety
- **Decision Deadline:** Before MVP launch

### OQ03: Minimum Order Values

- **Question:** Should platform enforce minimum order values per supplier or category?
- **Impact:** Medium - affects supplier/buyer mix, average order value
- **Options:**
    - No MOV (lower barrier to entry)
    - Supplier-defined MOV (flexible)
    - Platform-defined MOV (quality control)
- **Decision Owner:** VP Product
- **Decision Deadline:** Before supplier onboarding

### OQ04: International Expansion Priority

- **Question:** Which international market to enter first after US launch?
- **Impact:** High - affects localization, compliance, resource allocation
- **Options:**
    - United Kingdom (similar regulations, English language)
    - Germany (large market, complex compliance)
    - Singapore (gateway to Asia, complex compliance)
- **Decision Owner:** CEO / VP International
- **Decision Deadline:** Before Phase 2

### OQ05: White-Label Marketplace Option

- **Question:** Should platform offer white-label marketplace for enterprises?
- **Impact:** Medium - affects product roadmap, pricing model
- **Options:**
    - Multi-tenant only (standard product)
    - White-label add-on (premium pricing)
    - Dedicated instances (enterprise only)
- **Decision Owner:** VP Product / CRO
- **Decision Deadline:** Before Phase 3

### OQ06: Supplier Verification Depth

- **Question:** How deep should supplier verification go for different categories?
- **Impact:** Medium - affects onboarding time, supplier quality, liability
- **Options:**
    - Basic verification for all (fast onboarding)
    - Enhanced verification for high-risk categories
    - Full audit for all suppliers (slow onboarding)
- **Decision Owner:** VP Trust & Safety
- **Decision Deadline:** Before supplier onboarding

### OQ07: Pricing Model for Buyers

- **Question:** Should buyers pay subscription fees or use commission-only model?
- **Impact:** Medium - affects buyer acquisition, revenue predictability
- **Options:**
    - Free for buyers, revenue from suppliers only
    - Tiered buyer subscription
    - Transaction fee for buyers
- **Decision Owner:** CFO / CRO
- **Decision Deadline:** Before MVP launch

### OQ08: Data Ownership and Portability

- **Question:** Who owns marketplace data and how easily can buyers export it?
- **Impact:** Medium - affects buyer trust, competitive position
- **Options:**
    - Buyer owns all their data (full export)
    - Platform owns aggregated data
    - Shared ownership model
- **Decision Owner:** Legal / CPO
- **Decision Deadline:** Before terms of service finalization
